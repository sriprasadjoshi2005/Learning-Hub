## Learning objective

Establish the geometric facts needed for trigonometry, coordinate geometry and mensuration.

## Core concepts

- Angles on a straight line sum to 180°; angles around a point sum to 360°. Vertically opposite angles are equal.
- The interior angles of a triangle sum to 180°. An exterior angle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.
- For a triangle with sides a,b,c opposite angles A,B,C, the sine rule is a/sin A=b/sin B=c/sin C.
- The cosine rule is c²=a²+b²−2ab cos C. It generalises Pythagoras' theorem.
- The sum of interior angles of an n-sided polygon is (n−2)×180°. Each interior angle of a regular n-gon is (n−2)180°/n.

## Essential formulae and results

Triangle angle sum=180°; polygon interior-angle sum=(n−2)180°.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** A triangle has angles 50° and 60°; the third angle is 180°−110°=70°.
**Example 2.** A regular hexagon has each interior angle (6−2)180°/6=120°.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Use the included angle in the cosine rule.
- The exterior angle is not generally supplementary to both remote interior angles individually.
- Distinguish a regular polygon's interior angle from its exterior angle.

## Exam approach

1. Identify the topic and write down the relevant definition or formula.
2. Check the conditions: domain, non-zero denominators, interval, sign and units where applicable.
3. Show the essential algebraic steps instead of jumping to an answer.
4. Substitute the result into the original condition whenever extraneous solutions are possible.
5. Keep exact values (fractions, radicals and π) until a decimal approximation is requested.

## Quiz

1. The sum of angles in a triangle is:
- [ ] 90°
- [x] 180°
- [ ] 270°
- [ ] 360°
> This is a basic Euclidean geometry result.

2. Each interior angle of a regular pentagon is:
- [x] 108°
- [ ] 120°
- [ ] 135°
- [ ] 144°
> (5−2)×180°/5=108°.

3. In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is:
- [ ] The shortest side
- [ ] A side beside the right angle
- [x] Opposite the right angle
- [ ] Always equal to a leg
> The hypotenuse is the side opposite the 90° angle.
