# Mathematics syllabus plan (50 lessons, JEE Main / state-board level)

Each lesson: about 400 words of teaching, then a quiz of 3 hard questions with explanations.
Levels are set in each lesson's front matter. Plan: Foundation 1-10, Intermediate 11-26,
Advanced 27-47, Exam 48-50.

## Foundation (1-10)
01 Arithmetic Foundations and Order of Operations: BODMAS, number types, divisibility, HCF and LCM, estimation.
02 Integers, Fractions and Decimals: operations, recurring decimals as fractions, comparing and rationalising.
03 Ratio, Proportion, Percentages and Averages: direct and inverse proportion, percentage change, profit and loss, interest, mixtures, weighted averages.
04 Powers, Roots and Scientific Notation: laws of indices, surds and rationalisation, standard form.
05 Sets, Intervals and Basic Set Operations: notation, union, intersection, complement, Venn diagrams, n(A union B), intervals.
06 Algebraic Expressions and Identities: expansion, standard identities, cubes, factorisation.
07 Linear Equations and Word Problems: one and several unknowns, translating words, consistency.
08 Linear Inequalities and Absolute Value: solving and graphing, modulus equations and inequalities.
09 Polynomials and Factor Theorem: division, remainder and factor theorems, roots, relations between roots and coefficients.
10 Quadratic Equations and Graphs: formula, discriminant, nature of roots, sum and product, vertex and graph.

## Intermediate (11-26)
11 Functions, Domain and Range: relations, one-one and onto, composition, even and odd functions.
12 Graphs, Transformations and Inverse Functions: shifts, reflections, scaling, inverses, exponential and logarithmic functions and their laws.
13 Sequences and Arithmetic Progressions: nth term, sums, arithmetic mean.
14 Geometric Progressions and Series: GP, infinite GP, AM-GM inequality, sums of n, n squared, n cubed, arithmetico-geometric series.
15 Binomial Theorem: expansion, general and middle terms, coefficients, negative and fractional index.
16 Permutations and Combinations: counting principles, arrangements, selections, circular arrangements, repetition, distributions.
17 Complex Numbers: algebra, modulus and argument, polar form, De Moivre, roots of unity, geometry.
18 Quadratic Expressions, Roots and Inequality Sign Analysis: location of roots, wavy-curve method, rational inequalities, extreme values.
19 Matrices and Determinants: operations, transpose, inverse, determinant properties, Cramer's rule, consistency of systems.
20 Probability and Elementary Counting: classical probability, addition rule, conditional probability, independence, Bayes' theorem, binomial distribution.
21 Angles, Triangles and Polygon Geometry: angle facts, congruence and similarity, triangle centres, polygon angles, circle theorems, mensuration.
22 Trigonometric Ratios and Standard Angles: ratios, standard angles, quadrants, allied angles, radians.
23 Trigonometric Identities and Equations: sum and difference, multiple angles, product formulas, general solutions, sine and cosine rules, heights and distances.
24 Trigonometric Functions, Graphs and Inverse Trigonometry: periodicity, graphs, principal values, inverse identities.
25 Coordinate Geometry: Cartesian Plane and Distance: distance, section formula, areas, centroid, locus.
26 Straight Lines: slope and equation forms, angle between lines, distance from a point, families of lines, concurrency.

## Advanced (27-47)
27 Circles: equation forms, tangents and normals, chords, radical axis, families of circles.
28 Parabola: standard forms, focus and directrix, tangents and normals, parametric form.
29 Ellipse: standard form, eccentricity, tangents, normals, auxiliary circle.
30 Hyperbola: standard form, asymptotes, rectangular hyperbola, tangents.
31 Limits Foundations and Standard Limits: algebraic limits, indeterminate forms, standard limits, L'Hopital's rule, sandwich theorem.
32 Continuity and Differentiability: tests for continuity, types of discontinuity, differentiability.
33 Differentiation Rules and Standard Derivatives: product, quotient and chain rules, standard and inverse-trigonometric derivatives.
34 Implicit, Logarithmic and Parametric Differentiation: including higher-order derivatives.
35 Applications of Derivatives: Tangents, Normals and Rates: equations of tangent and normal, rates of change, approximations.
36 Applications of Derivatives: Monotonicity and Extrema: increasing and decreasing functions, maxima and minima, optimisation, Rolle and mean value theorems.
37 Antiderivatives and Indefinite Integration: standard integrals, substitution, trigonometric and algebraic integrals.
38 Integration by Parts and Partial Fractions: ILATE, partial fractions, special integrals.
39 Definite Integrals and Fundamental Theorem: properties, evaluation, symmetry, Leibniz rule.
40 Area Under Curves: areas between curves, areas involving conics.
41 Differential Equations: order and degree, separable, homogeneous and linear first-order equations, formation.
42 Differential Calculus and Integral Revision: mixed problems linking limits, derivatives and integrals.
43 Vectors: Algebra, Dot Product and Geometry: dot and cross products, triple products, projections, geometric applications.
44 Three-Dimensional Geometry: direction ratios, lines and planes, angles, distances, shortest distance.
45 Statistics: Mean, Variance and Standard Deviation: central tendency, dispersion, effect of changing data, grouped data.
46 Mathematical Reasoning and Logic: statements, connectives, truth tables, negation, contrapositive, quantifiers.
47 Number Theory: Divisibility, Remainders and Modular Arithmetic: congruences, remainders, units digits, factor counting.

## Exam (48-50)
48 Exam Problem Solving: Equations, Functions and Algebra: strategies, shortcuts, common traps.
49 Exam Problem Solving: Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry and Calculus: strategies, shortcuts, common traps.
50 Full Mathematics Mock Test and Revision Roadmap: mixed test and a revision plan.
