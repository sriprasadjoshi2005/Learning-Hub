## Learning objective

Develop derivative fluency using first principles, power, product, quotient and chain rules.

## Core concepts

- The derivative is the instantaneous rate of change and the slope of the tangent. From first principles, f'(x)=lim(h→0)[f(x+h)−f(x)]/h.
- Power rule: d(x^n)/dx=nx^(n−1). Constant multiple and sum rules allow term-by-term differentiation.
- Product rule: (uv)'=u'v+uv'. Quotient rule: (u/v)'=(u'v−uv')/v², v≠0.
- Chain rule: d[f(g(x))]/dx=f'(g(x))g'(x). Differentiate the outer function, retain the inner function, then multiply by the inner derivative.
- Standard derivatives: d(sin x)/dx=cos x; d(cos x)/dx=−sin x; d(e^x)/dx=e^x; d(ln x)/dx=1/x for x>0.

## Essential formulae and results

d(x^n)/dx=nx^(n−1); (uv)'=u'v+uv'; chain rule: [f(g(x))]'=f'(g(x))g'(x).

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** d(3x⁴−5x²+7)/dx=12x³−10x.
**Example 2.** For y=sin(x²), dy/dx=cos(x²)×2x=2x cos(x²).

## Common mistakes to avoid

- The derivative of a constant is zero.
- In the quotient rule, preserve the order u'v−uv'.
- Do not forget the inner derivative in a chain-rule problem.

## Exam approach

1. Identify the topic and write down the relevant definition or formula.
2. Check the conditions: domain, non-zero denominators, interval, sign and units where applicable.
3. Show the essential algebraic steps instead of jumping to an answer.
4. Substitute the result into the original condition whenever extraneous solutions are possible.
5. Keep exact values (fractions, radicals and π) until a decimal approximation is requested.

## Quiz

1. d(x⁵)/dx is:
- [ ] x⁴
- [x] 5x⁴
- [ ] 5x
- [ ] x⁶/6
> Apply the power rule.

2. d(cos x)/dx is:
- [ ] sin x
- [x] −sin x
- [ ] cos x
- [ ] −cos x
> The derivative of cosine is negative sine.

3. d(e^(3x))/dx is:
- [ ] e^(3x)
- [x] 3e^(3x)
- [ ] e^3x
- [ ] 3e^x
> Use the chain rule: derivative of 3x is 3.
