# Lesson 3: Ratio, Proportion, Percentages and Averages

## Learning objective

Use multiplicative comparisons to solve everyday and examination problems involving ratios, rates, percentages and averages.

## Core concepts

- A ratio a:b compares quantities in the same units. Simplify by dividing both terms by their greatest common divisor.
- If a:b = c:d, then ad = bc, provided the ratios are defined. In a direct proportion, y/x is constant; in an inverse proportion, xy is constant.
- p percent means p/100. A percentage increase by p% multiplies a quantity by (1 + p/100); a decrease multiplies it by (1 − p/100).
- Successive percentage changes multiply: a rise of 20% followed by a fall of 20% gives 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96, a net fall of 4%.
- Arithmetic mean = sum of observations ÷ number of observations. For two numbers, the mean lies halfway between them.

## Essential formulae and results

Percentage change multiplier = 1 ± p/100; mean = sum/count.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** Divide ₹840 in the ratio 3:4. Total parts = 7; one part = 840/7 = 120. The shares are ₹360 and ₹480.
**Example 2.** A price of ₹500 rises by 10%. New price = 500 × 1.10 = ₹550. A subsequent 10% discount gives 550 × 0.90 = ₹495.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- A 20% increase followed by a 20% decrease is not zero net change.
- Convert units before forming a ratio; 2 m : 50 cm = 200 : 50 = 4 : 1.
- Do not confuse the mean with the median or mode.

## Exam approach

1. Identify the topic and write down the relevant definition or formula.
2. Check the conditions: domain, non-zero denominators, interval, sign and units where applicable.
3. Show the essential algebraic steps instead of jumping to an answer.
4. Substitute the result into the original condition whenever extraneous solutions are possible.
5. Keep exact values (fractions, radicals and π) until a decimal approximation is requested.

## Quick quiz

**1. A quantity is divided in the ratio 2:3. What fraction is the smaller share?**
- [ ] 2/3
- [ ] 3/5
- [x] 2/5
- [ ] 1/2
> **Explanation:** There are five total parts, of which two belong to the smaller share.

**2. A value of 200 increases by 15%. What is the result?**
- [ ] 215
- [x] 230
- [ ] 235
- [ ] 170
> **Explanation:** 15% of 200 is 30; the new value is 230.

**3. The mean of 4, 8 and 12 is:**
- [ ] 6
- [ ] 7
- [x] 8
- [ ] 9
> **Explanation:** (4 + 8 + 12)/3 = 24/3 = 8.
