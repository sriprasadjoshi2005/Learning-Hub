## Learning objective

Use identities to simplify expressions and solve trigonometric equations over specified intervals.

## Core concepts

- Core identities include sin²x+cos²x=1, 1+tan²x=sec²x and 1+cot²x=cosec²x.
- Addition formulae: sin(A±B)=sin A cos B±cos A sin B; cos(A±B)=cos A cos B∓sin A sin B.
- Double-angle formulae include sin 2x=2sin x cos x and cos 2x=cos²x−sin²x=1−2sin²x=2cos²x−1.
- For sin x=sin α, general solutions are x=nπ+(-1)^nα, n∈Z. For cos x=cos α, x=2nπ±α. For tan x=tan α, x=nπ+α.
- When solving on a finite interval, list only values in that interval and respect whether endpoints are included.

## Essential formulae and results

sin²x+cos²x=1; sin2x=2sinx cosx; cos2x=cos²x−sin²x.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** Solve sin x=1/2 on [0,2π): x=π/6 or 5π/6.
**Example 2.** Simplify (1−cos²x)/sin x = sin²x/sin x = sin x, provided sin x≠0.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Squaring an equation can introduce extraneous solutions; check in the original equation.
- A trigonometric equation usually has infinitely many solutions unless an interval is specified.
- Check the period: sin and cos have period 2π; tan has period π.

## Exam approach

1. Identify the topic and write down the relevant definition or formula.
2. Check the conditions: domain, non-zero denominators, interval, sign and units where applicable.
3. Show the essential algebraic steps instead of jumping to an answer.
4. Substitute the result into the original condition whenever extraneous solutions are possible.
5. Keep exact values (fractions, radicals and π) until a decimal approximation is requested.

## Quiz

1. The period of tan x is:
- [ ] π/2
- [x] π
- [ ] 2π
- [ ] 4π
> tan(x+π)=tan x.

2. sin 2x equals:
- [x] 2sin x cos x
- [ ] sin²x−cos²x
- [ ] 2cos²x
- [ ] 1−sin x
> This is the sine double-angle identity.

3. Solutions of cos x=0 in [0,2π) are:
- [ ] 0, π
- [x] π/2, 3π/2
- [ ] π/2 only
- [ ] π, 2π
> Cosine is zero at the two odd quarter-turns in this interval.
