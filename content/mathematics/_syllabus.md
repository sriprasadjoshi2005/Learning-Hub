# Mathematics syllabus plan (50 lessons, foundation to JEE Main / state-board and entrance-exam level)

Foundations 1-10: arithmetic and order of operations, integers, fractions and decimals, ratios and percentages, powers and roots, sets and intervals, algebraic expressions and identities, linear equations and inequalities, polynomials, and quadratic equations.

Core algebra 11-20: functions, graphs and inverse functions, sequences and progressions, binomial theorem, permutations and combinations, complex numbers, quadratic inequalities, matrices and determinants, and probability.

Trigonometry and geometry 21-30: elementary geometry, trigonometric ratios and identities, trigonometric equations and graphs, inverse trigonometry, Cartesian coordinates, straight lines, circles, and the conic sections parabola, ellipse and hyperbola.

Calculus 31-42: limits, continuity, differentiability, differentiation techniques, applications of derivatives, antiderivatives, integration techniques, definite integrals, area under curves, differential equations, and calculus revision.

Exam preparation 43-50: vectors, three-dimensional geometry, statistics, mathematical reasoning and logic, number theory, mixed algebra and function problem-solving, mixed trigonometry/coordinate-geometry/calculus problem-solving, and a full mathematics mock test with a revision roadmap.

The sequence begins with school-level prerequisites and builds toward problems commonly encountered in board, state-level entrance and JEE Main preparation. Check the latest official syllabus for the examination being attempted; this learning plan is a broad study pathway, not an official examination syllabus.
