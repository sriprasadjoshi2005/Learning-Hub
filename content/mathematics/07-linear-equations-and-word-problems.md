# Lesson 7: Linear Equations and Word Problems

## Learning objective

Learn to solve one-variable linear equations and translate verbal information into equations.

## Core concepts

- An equation states that two expressions are equal. The solution is a value that makes the equality true.
- Whatever operation is performed on one side must be performed on the other. This preserves equality.
- For ax+b=c, if a≠0, then x=(c−b)/a.
- For word problems, define the unknown, express the relationships, form an equation, solve and check the result in the original context.
- A formula can be rearranged like an equation. For example, v=u+at gives t=(v−u)/a when a≠0.

## Essential formulae and results

ax+b=c ⇒ x=(c−b)/a, provided a≠0.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** Solve 3x−7=11. Add 7: 3x=18. Divide by 3: x=6. Check: 18−7=11.
**Example 2.** A number is 5 more than twice another. If the smaller number is x, the larger is 2x+5; the statement gives a relationship that can be used to form an equation.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Do not move a term across the equals sign without changing its operation correctly.
- Clear fractions by multiplying every term on both sides by the common denominator.
- A negative coefficient must be divided with its sign.

## Exam approach

1. Identify the topic and write down the relevant definition or formula.
2. Check the conditions: domain, non-zero denominators, interval, sign and units where applicable.
3. Show the essential algebraic steps instead of jumping to an answer.
4. Substitute the result into the original condition whenever extraneous solutions are possible.
5. Keep exact values (fractions, radicals and π) until a decimal approximation is requested.

## Quick quiz

**1. Solve 4x+3=19.**
- [ ] 3
- [x] 4
- [ ] 5
- [ ] 6
> **Explanation:** Subtract 3 to get 4x=16, hence x=4.

**2. Solve 2(x−3)=10.**
- [ ] 2
- [ ] 5
- [x] 8
- [ ] 13
> **Explanation:** Divide by 2: x−3=5, so x=8.

**3. If x/3=7, x equals:**
- [ ] 10
- [ ] 14
- [x] 21
- [ ] 28
> **Explanation:** Multiply both sides by 3.
