# Lesson 1: Arithmetic Foundations and Order of Operations

## Learning objective

Build fluency with the four operations, place value, brackets and the order in which calculations are performed. These skills are prerequisites for every later topic.

## Core concepts

- A digit's value depends on its place: in 4,582, the 5 represents 500, not 5.
- For addition and subtraction, align place values. For multiplication, multiply each place-value contribution and add the partial products.
- For division, interpret a/b as the number which multiplied by b gives a, provided b is non-zero.
- Use BODMAS/PEMDAS carefully: brackets, powers, multiplication and division from left to right, then addition and subtraction from left to right. Multiplication does not always precede division.

## Essential formulae and results

Order of operations: brackets → powers → multiplication/division left to right → addition/subtraction left to right.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** Evaluate 18 − 3 × (4 + 2). First calculate the bracket: 4 + 2 = 6. Then 3 × 6 = 18. Finally 18 − 18 = 0.
**Example 2.** Evaluate 48 ÷ 6 × 2. Division and multiplication have equal precedence, so work left to right: 8 × 2 = 16.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Treating multiplication as always before division, rather than using left-to-right order.
- Ignoring a negative sign or applying it to only part of a bracket.
- Rounding intermediate values too early.

## Exam approach

1. Identify the topic and write down the relevant definition or formula.
2. Check the conditions: domain, non-zero denominators, interval, sign and units where applicable.
3. Show the essential algebraic steps instead of jumping to an answer.
4. Substitute the result into the original condition whenever extraneous solutions are possible.
5. Keep exact values (fractions, radicals and π) until a decimal approximation is requested.

## Quick quiz

**1. What is 7 + 3 × 4?**
- [ ] 40
- [x] 19
- [ ] 28
- [ ] 10
> **Explanation:** Multiplication first: 7 + 12 = 19.

**2. Evaluate 36 ÷ 3 × 2.**
- [ ] 6
- [x] 24
- [ ] 18
- [ ] 12
> **Explanation:** Work left to right: 12 × 2 = 24.

**3. What is the value of 5² − 4 × 3?**
- [x] 13
- [ ] 9
- [ ] 21
- [ ] 1
> **Explanation:** The square is 25 and the product is 12; 25 − 12 = 13.
