# Lesson 28: Parabola

## Learning objective

Understand the standard parabola, focus-directrix definition, latus rectum and tangent equations.

## Core concepts

- A parabola is the locus of a point whose distance from a fixed focus equals its perpendicular distance from a fixed directrix.
- For y²=4ax, vertex is (0,0), focus (a,0), directrix x=−a, axis is the x-axis and latus rectum length is 4a.
- For x²=4ay, focus is (0,a), directrix y=−a and axis is the y-axis. Sign changes reverse the opening direction.
- The tangent to y²=4ax at (x₁,y₁) is yy₁=2a(x+x₁). The tangent to x²=4ay is xx₁=2a(y+y₁).
- A point lies on the parabola only if it satisfies its equation; verify before using the tangent formula.

## Essential formulae and results

y²=4ax: focus (a,0), directrix x=−a, latus rectum length 4a.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** For y²=12x, 4a=12 so a=3. Focus=(3,0), directrix x=−3, latus rectum length=12.
**Example 2.** For y²=8x at point (2,4), the tangent is 4y=4(x+2), hence y=x+2.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Identify whether the squared variable is x or y before locating the axis.
- The parameter a is one-fourth of the coefficient on the right side.
- Use coordinates that satisfy the curve equation in tangent formulas.

## Quick quiz

**1. For y²=20x, the focus is:**
- [x] (5,0)
- [ ] (0,5)
- [ ] (−5,0)
- [ ] (0,−5)
> **Explanation:** 4a=20 gives a=5 and focus (a,0).

**2. The length of the latus rectum of x²=4ay is:**
- [ ] a
- [ ] 2a
- [x] 4a
- [ ] 8a
> **Explanation:** The standard latus rectum length is 4a.

**3. The directrix of y²=16x is:**
- [ ] x=4
- [x] x=−4
- [ ] y=−4
- [ ] y=4
> **Explanation:** Here a=4, so directrix is x=−a=−4.
