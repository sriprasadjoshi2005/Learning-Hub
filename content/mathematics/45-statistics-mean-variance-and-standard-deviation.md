# Lesson 45: Statistics: Mean, Variance and Standard Deviation

## Learning objective

Summarise numerical data using measures of central tendency and spread, including frequency distributions.

## Core concepts

- Arithmetic mean for n observations is x̄=Σxᵢ/n. With frequencies fᵢ, x̄=Σfᵢxᵢ/Σfᵢ.
- Variance is the mean of squared deviations from the mean: σ²=Σ(xᵢ−x̄)²/n for a population. Standard deviation is σ=√σ².
- An equivalent population formula is σ²=Σxᵢ²/n−x̄². For frequency data, weight each term by its frequency.
- Median is the middle ordered value (or average of two middle values); mode is the most frequent value.
- Variance is measured in squared units; standard deviation has the original data units.

## Essential formulae and results

Population variance=Σ(xᵢ−x̄)²/n; standard deviation=√variance.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** For 2,4,6, mean=4. Squared deviations are 4,0,4; population variance=8/3 and standard deviation=√(8/3).
**Example 2.** For values 1,2,2,5, the mode is 2 and the median is (2+2)/2=2.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Do not divide by n−1 when the question asks for population variance; sample variance uses a different convention.
- Sort data before finding the median.
- Standard deviation is the square root of variance, not variance itself.

## Quick quiz

**1. The mean of 2, 4, 6, 8 is:**
- [ ] 4
- [x] 5
- [ ] 6
- [ ] 20
> **Explanation:** The sum is 20 and there are four observations.

**2. Standard deviation is:**
- [ ] Variance squared
- [x] Square root of variance
- [ ] Mean squared
- [ ] Range divided by 2
> **Explanation:** By definition, standard deviation is the non-negative square root of variance.

**3. The median of 1, 3, 7 is:**
- [ ] 1
- [x] 3
- [ ] 7
- [ ] 11/3
> **Explanation:** The middle value after ordering is 3.
