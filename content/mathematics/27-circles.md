# Lesson 27: Circles

## Learning objective

Derive circle equations and solve problems involving centre, radius, tangents and chord properties.

## Core concepts

- A circle is the locus of points at a fixed distance r from a centre (h,k). Its equation is (x−h)²+(y−k)²=r².
- The general equation x²+y²+2gx+2fy+c=0 has centre (−g,−f) and radius √(g²+f²−c), provided the radius is real.
- A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact. For x²+y²=r², the tangent at (x₁,y₁) on the circle is xx₁+yy₁=r².
- The perpendicular from the centre to a chord bisects the chord. Equal chords are equidistant from the centre.
- A line is tangent when its distance from the centre equals the radius.

## Essential formulae and results

Circle: (x−h)²+(y−k)²=r².

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** For x²+y²−6x+4y−12=0, centre=(3,−2), radius=√(9+4+12)=5.
**Example 2.** For x²+y²=25, tangent at (3,4) is 3x+4y=25.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Complete squares carefully when finding centre and radius.
- A point must lie on the circle for the point-tangent formula to apply.
- A line at distance less than r cuts the circle; equal to r is tangent; greater than r does not meet it.

## Quick quiz

**1. The centre of (x−2)²+(y+3)²=16 is:**
- [ ] (2,3)
- [ ] (−2,3)
- [x] (2,−3)
- [ ] (−2,−3)
> **Explanation:** The standard form gives centre (h,k)=(2,−3).

**2. The radius of x²+y²=49 is:**
- [x] 7
- [ ] 49
- [ ] 14
- [ ] √7
> **Explanation:** The radius is √49=7.

**3. A tangent to a circle at a point is perpendicular to:**
- [ ] A chord
- [ ] The diameter anywhere
- [x] The radius at contact
- [ ] The x-axis
> **Explanation:** The radius to the point of contact is normal to the tangent.
