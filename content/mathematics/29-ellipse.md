# Lesson 29: Ellipse

## Learning objective

Study the ellipse as a conic with two foci, and derive its standard elements and eccentricity.

## Core concepts

- An ellipse is the locus for which the sum of distances from two fixed foci is constant.
- For x²/a²+y²/b²=1 with a>b>0, the major axis is along x, c²=a²−b², foci are (±c,0), and eccentricity e=c/a.
- For y²/a²+x²/b²=1, the major axis is along y and foci are (0,±c).
- The major-axis length is 2a, minor-axis length is 2b, and 0<e<1 for a non-degenerate ellipse.
- The latus rectum length is 2b²/a. A circle is a special ellipse with a=b and e=0.

## Essential formulae and results

Ellipse: c²=a²−b²; e=c/a; latus rectum=2b²/a.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** For x²/25+y²/9=1, a=5, b=3, c=√(25−9)=4, foci=(±4,0), e=4/5.
**Example 2.** The major and minor axis lengths in the same ellipse are 10 and 6.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- The larger denominator identifies a² and the major axis.
- For a vertical major axis, place foci on the y-axis.
- Do not use c²=a²+b²; that relation belongs to a hyperbola.

## Quick quiz

**1. For x²/25+y²/9=1, the eccentricity is:**
- [ ] 3/5
- [x] 4/5
- [ ] 5/4
- [ ] 1/5
> **Explanation:** c=4 and a=5, so e=4/5.

**2. An ellipse has eccentricity:**
- [ ] Greater than 1
- [ ] Equal to 1
- [x] Between 0 and 1
- [ ] Negative
> **Explanation:** A non-degenerate ellipse has 0≤e<1; for a non-circular ellipse, 0<e<1.

**3. The major-axis length for a=7 is:**
- [ ] 7
- [x] 14
- [ ] 49
- [ ] 28
> **Explanation:** The full major axis is 2a.
