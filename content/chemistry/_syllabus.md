# Chemistry syllabus plan (50 lessons, JEE Main / state-board level)

Foundation 1-10: atoms, isotopes, mole, limiting reagent, concentration, redox, Bohr model,
quantum numbers, periodic trends, bonding, VSEPR and hybridisation.

Intermediate 11-28: MO theory, gaseous state, real gases, thermodynamics (3), equilibrium (2),
acids/bases/pH, buffers and Ksp, colligative properties, electrochemistry (2), kinetics (2),
solid state, s-block, p-block groups 13-14.

Advanced 29-44: p-block groups 15-18, d- and f-block, coordination compounds (2), metallurgy,
organic nomenclature and isomerism, electronic effects, hydrocarbons, aromatic chemistry,
haloalkanes, alcohols/phenols/ethers, carbonyls, acids and derivatives, amines, biomolecules,
polymers and everyday chemistry.

Exam 45-50: stereochemistry, named reactions and reagents, multi-step organic problems,
physical-chemistry numerical strategies, inorganic exceptions and traps, mixed JEE-style problems.
