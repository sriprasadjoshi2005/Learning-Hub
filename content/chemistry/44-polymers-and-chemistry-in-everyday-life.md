## Polymers

Polymers are giant molecules built from repeating units (monomers). They are classified by how they form.

**Addition polymers** form from monomers with a C=C bond, with no byproduct: polythene, PVC (from $\mathrm{CH_2{=}CHCl}$), PTFE (Teflon), polystyrene, polyacrylonitrile, and natural rubber (**cis-polyisoprene**). Low-density polythene is made by a free-radical process at high pressure; high-density polythene uses Ziegler-Natta catalysts at low pressure.

**Condensation polymers** form with loss of a small molecule such as water:

| Polymer | Monomers |
|---|---|
| Nylon-6,6 | hexane-1,6-diamine + hexanedioic (adipic) acid |
| Nylon-6 | caprolactam (ring-opening) |
| Terylene (PET) | ethylene glycol + terephthalic acid |
| Bakelite | phenol + methanal |
| Melamine-formaldehyde | melamine + methanal |

**Copolymers** use more than one monomer (Buna-S, Buna-N). **Vulcanisation** heats natural rubber with sulfur, creating cross-links that improve elasticity and strength. Biodegradable polymers such as PHBV break down in the environment.

## Molar mass of polymers

A polymer sample has chains of different lengths, so there are two averages: the **number average** $\overline{M}_n = \dfrac{\sum N_iM_i}{\sum N_i}$ and the **weight average** $\overline{M}_w = \dfrac{\sum N_iM_i^2}{\sum N_iM_i}$. Always $\overline{M}_w \geq \overline{M}_n$, with equality only if all chains are the same length.

## Chemistry in everyday life

- **Analgesics** (aspirin, paracetamol) relieve pain; **antibiotics** kill or inhibit microbes; **antihistamines** treat allergies; **antacids** neutralise excess stomach acid; **tranquilisers** treat anxiety.
- **Antiseptics** can be applied to living tissue (dilute phenol, iodine); **disinfectants** are used on inanimate objects.
- **Detergents** are synthetic cleansing agents that work in hard water, unlike soaps, because their calcium and magnesium salts are soluble. **Branched** hydrocarbon chains resist bacterial breakdown, so linear chains are used to make them biodegradable.

## Quiz

1. Which pair of monomers gives nylon-6,6?
   - [ ] Caprolactam alone
   - [x] Hexane-1,6-diamine and hexanedioic acid
   - [ ] Ethylene glycol and terephthalic acid
   - [ ] Phenol and methanal
   > Nylon-6,6 is a polyamide formed by condensation between a diamine and a diacid, each with six carbons. The other three options give nylon-6, terylene and Bakelite, respectively.

2. A polymer sample has $100$ chains of molar mass $10\,000$ and $100$ chains of molar mass $30\,000$ g mol$^{-1}$. What are $\overline{M}_n$ and $\overline{M}_w$?
   - [x] 20 000 and 25 000
   - [ ] 25 000 and 20 000
   - [ ] 20 000 and 20 000
   - [ ] 30 000 and 25 000
   > $\overline{M}_n = (100\times10^4 + 100\times3\times10^4)/200 = 20\,000$. $\overline{M}_w = (100\times10^8 + 100\times9\times10^8)/(4\times10^6) = 25\,000$. The weight average is always the larger, since heavy chains count more.

3. What is the main purpose of vulcanising natural rubber?
   - [ ] To make it biodegradable
   - [ ] To make it water-soluble
   - [ ] To increase its molar mass by condensation with water
   - [x] To form sulfur cross-links that increase its elasticity and strength
   > Raw rubber is soft and sticky. Sulfur bridges between chains stop them sliding past each other permanently, so the material returns to its shape when stretched.
