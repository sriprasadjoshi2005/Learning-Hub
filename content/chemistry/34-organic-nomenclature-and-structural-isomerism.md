level: advanced

Organic chemistry rests on carbon's ability to form four covalent bonds and long chains. Compounds are grouped by **functional group**, the atom or group that decides the chemistry.

## IUPAC naming

1. Find the **principal functional group** (highest priority) and the longest chain that contains it.
2. Number the chain to give the principal group the **lowest locant**, then multiple bonds, then substituents.
3. Name substituents alphabetically, with locants and prefixes (di-, tri-).

Priority, highest first: carboxylic acid $>$ sulfonic acid $>$ ester $>$ acid halide $>$ amide $>$ nitrile $>$ aldehyde $>$ ketone $>$ alcohol $>$ amine $>$ alkene $>$ alkyne. Halogens, nitro and alkyl groups are only prefixes.

## Degree of unsaturation

The number of rings plus $\pi$ bonds is

$$\text{DBE} = C - \frac{H + X}{2} + \frac{N}{2} + 1$$

where $X$ is the number of halogens. Oxygen and sulfur do not appear. For benzene, $\mathrm{C_6H_6}$: $6 - 3 + 1 = 4$ (one ring and three $\pi$ bonds).

## Structural isomers

Compounds with the same molecular formula and different connectivity:

- **Chain isomers:** different carbon skeletons (butane and 2-methylpropane).
- **Position isomers:** the same skeleton, different position of a group (propan-1-ol and propan-2-ol).
- **Functional isomers:** different functional groups (ethanol and methoxymethane).
- **Metamers:** different alkyl groups on either side of the same functional group (diethyl ether and methyl propyl ether).
- **Tautomers:** rapidly interconverting isomers, such as keto and enol forms.

To count isomers of a formula, work systematically: first fix the carbon skeleton, then place the functional group, and finally check for duplicates. Such questions are common in JEE.

## Quiz

1. What is the degree of unsaturation of nitrobenzene, $\mathrm{C_6H_5NO_2}$?
   - [ ] 4
   - [ ] 6
   - [ ] 7
   - [x] 5
   > $\text{DBE} = 6 - 5/2 + 1/2 + 1 = 5$. This corresponds to the ring and three C=C bonds of the benzene ring (four) plus the N=O bond of the nitro group (one).

2. What is the IUPAC name of $\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CHCH_2CH(OH)CH_3}$?
   - [ ] 2-Methylpentan-4-ol
   - [ ] 4-Methylpentan-3-ol
   - [x] 4-Methylpentan-2-ol
   - [ ] 1,3-Dimethylbutan-1-ol
   > Number from the end nearest the $\mathrm{OH}$ so the principal group gets the lowest locant: the OH is on C2 and the methyl on C4. Numbering from the other end would give 2-methylpentan-4-ol, which has the wrong locant for the OH group.

3. How many structural isomers (alcohols and ethers together) have the molecular formula $\mathrm{C_4H_{10}O}$?
   - [ ] 4
   - [x] 7
   - [ ] 5
   - [ ] 8
   > Alcohols: butan-1-ol, butan-2-ol, 2-methylpropan-1-ol and 2-methylpropan-2-ol (4). Ethers: methoxypropane, 2-methoxypropane and ethoxyethane (3). Total 7.
