# Mathematics syllabus plan (50 lessons, foundation to university entrance-exam level)

**Phase 1: Foundations (Lessons 1–12):** Number systems and mathematical fundamentals, basic arithmetic and operations, fractions, decimals and percentages, ratios and proportions, powers and exponents, square roots and cube roots, factors and multiples, prime factorization, HCF and LCM, introduction to algebra, algebraic expressions, linear equations and inequalities, basic geometry, angles and geometric properties, mensuration, perimeter, area and volume, Cartesian coordinates, introduction to graphs, mathematical patterns, sequences, and fundamental mathematical reasoning and problem-solving.

**Phase 2: Intermediate mathematics (Lessons 13–26):** Polynomials and algebraic identities, factorization and algebraic fractions, quadratic equations and their applications, simultaneous linear equations, advanced inequalities and absolute value, functions and relations, domain and range, composition and inverse functions, graphs and transformations of functions, arithmetic and geometric progressions, sequences and series, logarithms and exponential functions, triangle and circle geometry, coordinate geometry of straight lines and circles, trigonometric ratios and the unit circle, trigonometric identities and equations, permutations, combinations and fundamental counting principles.

**Phase 3: Advanced mathematics and analysis (Lessons 27–40):** Limits and continuity, differential calculus and differentiation rules, advanced differentiation and implicit differentiation, applications of derivatives including tangents, normals, rates of change, maxima and minima, integral calculus and integration techniques, definite integrals and their applications, differential equations, conic sections including parabola, ellipse and hyperbola, three-dimensional geometry, vectors and vector algebra, matrices and determinants, probability theory including conditional probability and Bayes' theorem, statistics and data analysis, complex numbers, polar representation and De Moivre's theorem.

**Phase 4: Examination mastery and mocks (Lessons 41–50):** Advanced algebra and equation-solving techniques, advanced trigonometry and coordinate geometry, advanced calculus problem-solving, advanced probability and combinatorics, mathematical reasoning and quantitative aptitude, integrated multi-concept problem-solving, advanced mathematical problem-solving strategies and techniques, previous-year university entrance examination questions and analysis, full-length Mathematics mock examination I, and full-length Mathematics mock examination II with comprehensive solutions and performance analysis.

The sequence begins with fundamental arithmetic, number sense and elementary algebra, progressively develops mathematical reasoning and problem-solving abilities, and advances toward higher secondary and university-level mathematics, including calculus, probability, vectors, matrices and complex numbers. The final phase emphasizes challenging, time-constrained and integrated problems commonly encountered in university entrance and competitive examinations such as JEE Main, JEE Advanced and CUET, while also providing a foundation for other mathematics-based university admission tests. Check the latest official syllabus for the examination being attempted; this learning plan is a broad mathematical study pathway, not an official examination syllabus.
