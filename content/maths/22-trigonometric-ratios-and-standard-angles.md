## Learning objective

Introduce sine, cosine and tangent through right triangles, then build exact values for standard angles.

## Core concepts

- In a right triangle, sin θ=opposite/hypotenuse, cos θ=adjacent/hypotenuse and tan θ=opposite/adjacent.
- Reciprocal ratios are cosec θ=1/sin θ, sec θ=1/cos θ and cot θ=1/tan θ, wherever defined.
- Memorise exact values at 0°, 30°, 45°, 60° and 90°. For example, sin 30°=1/2, cos 60°=1/2 and tan 45°=1.
- The fundamental identity is sin²θ+cos²θ=1. Dividing by cos²θ gives 1+tan²θ=sec²θ where cos θ≠0.
- Use degrees or radians consistently; calculus formulas normally use radians.

## Essential formulae and results

sinθ=opposite/hypotenuse; cosθ=adjacent/hypotenuse; tanθ=opposite/adjacent.

## Worked examples

**Example 1.** In a 3-4-5 right triangle, for the angle opposite the side of length 3, sin θ=3/5 and cos θ=4/5.
**Example 2.** If sin θ=3/5 and θ is acute, cos θ=4/5 and tan θ=3/4.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- The opposite and adjacent sides depend on the chosen angle.
- tan 90° is undefined because cos 90°=0.
- Do not use degree values in a radian-based calculus formula without conversion.

## Exam approach

1. Identify the topic and write down the relevant definition or formula.
2. Check the conditions: domain, non-zero denominators, interval, sign and units where applicable.
3. Show the essential algebraic steps instead of jumping to an answer.
4. Substitute the result into the original condition whenever extraneous solutions are possible.
5. Keep exact values (fractions, radicals and π) until a decimal approximation is requested.

## Quiz

1. sin 30° equals:
- [ ] √3/2
- [x] 1/2
- [ ] 1
- [ ] 0
> The standard exact value is one-half.

2. tan 45° equals:
- [ ] 0
- [ ] 1/2
- [x] 1
- [ ] √3
> At 45°, opposite and adjacent legs are equal.

3. If sin θ=3/5 and θ is acute, cos θ is:
- [ ] 3/5
- [x] 4/5
- [ ] 5/3
- [ ] 1/5
> Use sin²θ+cos²θ=1 and choose the positive root.
