Computer Science is the study of computation, information processing, algorithms, programming and the design of computer systems. Artificial Intelligence is a major area of computer science concerned with creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence.

## 1. Meaning of Computer Science

Computer Science involves the systematic study of:

- Computation and information processing.
- Algorithms and problem-solving.
- Programming languages and software development.
- Computer hardware and architecture.
- Data storage, retrieval and communication.
- Theoretical foundations of computing.

A computer system receives data, processes it according to instructions, stores information and produces useful output.

## 2. Meaning of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the development of computer systems that can perform tasks associated with intelligent behaviour.

Examples include:

- Recognising objects in images.
- Understanding human language.
- Recommending movies and products.
- Playing strategic games.
- Predicting patterns from historical data.
- Generating text, images and other content.

AI systems may use rules, search algorithms, machine learning or other computational techniques.

## 3. Relationship Between Computer Science and AI

Computer Science provides the foundations on which AI is developed.

| Computer Science | Artificial Intelligence |
|---|---|
| Studies computation and algorithms | Develops systems that perform intelligent tasks |
| Includes programming and databases | Includes machine learning and reasoning |
| Designs general-purpose software | Develops intelligent applications |
| Studies computer architecture | Uses computational resources for AI models |

AI is a subfield of computer science, but it also draws ideas from mathematics, statistics, neuroscience, linguistics and philosophy.

## 4. Major Areas of Computer Science

1. Programming: Writing instructions for computers.
2. Algorithms: Designing systematic problem-solving procedures.
3. Data Structures: Organising information efficiently.
4. Computer Architecture: Understanding hardware organisation.
5. Operating Systems: Managing hardware and software resources.
6. Databases: Storing and managing structured information.
7. Computer Networks: Connecting devices for communication.
8. Cybersecurity: Protecting systems and information.
9. Software Engineering: Developing and maintaining software.
10. Artificial Intelligence: Building systems capable of intelligent behaviour.

## 5. Applications of Computer Science and AI

- Healthcare: Medical image analysis and diagnostic assistance.
- Education: Adaptive learning and automated assessment.
- Finance: Fraud detection and risk analysis.
- Transportation: Navigation and autonomous driving research.
- Agriculture: Crop monitoring and yield prediction.
- Entertainment: Recommendation systems and content generation.
- Industry: Robotics and automated quality inspection.

## 6. Limitations and Challenges

Computer systems do not automatically possess human understanding or judgement.

Important challenges include:

- Incorrect or incomplete data.
- Security vulnerabilities.
- Algorithmic bias.
- Privacy concerns.
- High computational requirements.
- Dependence on human-designed objectives and instructions.

## Common Exam Traps

- Computer Science is broader than programming alone.
- AI is a subfield of Computer Science, not a replacement for it.
- Automation does not necessarily involve AI.
- Machine learning is one approach within AI, not the entirety of AI.
- A computer processes information according to its hardware and software design.
