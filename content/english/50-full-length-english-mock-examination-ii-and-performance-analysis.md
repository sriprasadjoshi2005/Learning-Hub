## Examination Questions

### Section 1: Advanced Syntax and Mechanics

1. Select the sentence that is completely free of grammatical, modifier, or punctuation errors.
   - (A) Rarely we have seen such a perspicacious analysis of economic trends.
   - (B) Rarely have we seen such a perspicacious analysis of economic trends.
   - (C) Rarely have we saw such a perspicacious analysis of economic trends.
   - (D) Rarely we saw such a perspicacious analysis of economic trends.

2. Choose the option that correctly rectifies the faulty comparison: *"The energy output of nuclear reactors is significantly greater than solar panels."*
   - (A) The energy output of nuclear reactors is significantly greater than solar panels' reactors.
   - (B) The energy output of nuclear reactors is significantly greater than that of solar panels.
   - (C) The energy output of nuclear reactors is significantly greater than those of solar panels.
   - (D) Energy output of nuclear reactors are greater than solar panels.

3. Identify the correct sentence using non-finite verbs and prepositions.
   - (A) The team is committed to expand the research facility next spring.
   - (B) The team is committed to expanding the research facility next spring.
   - (C) The team is committed for expanding the research facility next spring.
   - (D) The team is committed to expansion of research facility next spring.

### Section 2: Vocabulary, Nuance, and Cloze

4. Complete the sentence: *"Unwilling to accept the \_\_\_\_\_ of the preliminary data, the director ordered a second \_\_\_\_\_ audit of the lab procedures."*
   - (A) clarity; casual
   - (B) equivocalness; rigorous
   - (C) certainty; superficial
   - (D) perfection; hurried

5. Identify the word with the most **negative** connotation among the near-synonyms for "saving money".
   - (A) Frugal
   - (B) Thrifty
   - (C) Miserly
   - (D) Economical

### Section 3: Critical Reasoning and Logic

6. *Argument:* "All members of the ethics committee voted in favor of the transparency policy. Dr. Aris is a member of the ethics committee. Therefore, Dr. Aris voted in favor of the transparency policy." What type of reasoning is demonstrated?
   - (A) Fallacious Post Hoc reasoning
   - (B) Valid deductive reasoning (Syllogism)
   - (C) Circular reasoning (Begging the question)
   - (D) Weak inductive extrapolation

7. Identify the implicit, unstated assumption in: *"Implementing mandatory anti-glare filters on all office monitors will reduce employee eye strain."*
   - (A) Anti-glare filters are manufactured domestically.
   - (B) Office monitors are the primary cause of employee eye strain.
   - (C) Employees prefer working on modern monitors.
   - (D) Eye strain is the only health issue facing office workers.

### Section 4: Dual Passage Comparative Analysis

*Passage 1 Excerpt:*
> "Technological automation represents the ultimate engine of economic liberation. By delegating repetitive manufacturing tasks to artificial intelligence and robotics, society reduces human labor costs and unlocks human creative potential for higher-order intellectual pursuits."

*Passage 2 Excerpt:*
> "While automation increases industrial efficiency, it threatens severe structural unemployment among low-skilled labor pools. Without robust social safety nets and continuous retraining programs, rapid technological integration risks widening the economic divide between capital owners and displaced workers."

8. What is the central point of departure between the author of Passage 1 and the author of Passage 2?
   - (A) Whether robotics increases manufacturing productivity
   - (B) The broader societal and employment impact of technological automation
   - (C) The cost of microchip fabrication
   - (D) Whether human beings prefer creative tasks to manual labor

9. How would the author of Passage 2 most likely respond to Passage 1's claim that automation "unlocks human creative potential"?
   - (A) By offering total agreement without qualification
   - (B) By contending that displaced workers cannot access creative roles without retraining and support
   - (C) By arguing that artificial intelligence is incapable of performing repetitive tasks
   - (D) By suggesting that manufacturing should be banned completely

10. Which underlying assumption is shared by BOTH authors despite their differing outlooks?
    - (A) Automation has zero impact on industrial efficiency.
    - (B) Technological automation is significantly altering contemporary labor structures.
    - (C) Retraining programs are completely ineffective.
    - (D) Low-skilled labor should be eliminated entirely.

## Answer Key & Diagnostic Explanations

1. **(B)** Negative adverbial "Rarely" requires subject-auxiliary inversion ($\text{Rarely} + \text{have} + \text{we} + \text{seen}$).
2. **(B)** Singular attribute ("energy output") requires "that of" when comparing nuclear reactors to solar panels.
3. **(B)** The adjective phrase "committed to" requires a gerund object ("expanding").
4. **(B)** Negative/ambiguous initial data ("equivocalness") logically prompts a thorough ("rigorous") audit.
5. **(C)** "Miserly" carries a strong negative connotation of stinginess, unlike positive terms like "frugal" or "thrifty".
6. **(B)** Follows a classic, valid categorical syllogism ($\text{All } A \text{ are } B; C \text{ is } A \implies C \text{ is } B$).
7. **(B)** For filters on monitors to reduce eye strain, monitors must be a primary cause of that strain.
8. **(B)** Passage 1 focuses on economic liberation; Passage 2 highlights structural unemployment and economic division.
9. **(B)** Passage 2 explicitly states that without retraining and safety nets, displaced workers face unemployment rather than smooth transitions.
10. **(B)** Both authors acknowledge that technological automation is deeply transforming labor and work structures.
