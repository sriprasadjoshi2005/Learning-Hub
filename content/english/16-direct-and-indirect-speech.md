Direct and indirect speech (reported speech) are structural conventions used to convey what someone has spoken or written. Converting direct speech into indirect speech involves systematically adjusting tenses, pronouns, demonstratives, and time/place adverbs.

## Main Concept 1: Principles of Reported Speech

- **Direct Speech:** Reports the exact words spoken, enclosed within quotation marks (*She said, "I am studying."*).
- **Indirect Speech (Reported Speech):** Reports the essential message without exact quotation marks, introduced by a reporting clause (*She said that she was studying.*).
- **Reporting Verbs:** Common verbs include *say, tell, state, inquire, ask, claim, order, explain*. Note that *tell* requires an indirect object (*He told **me** that...*), whereas *say* does not (*He said that...*).

## Main Concept 2: Systematic Backshifting and Deictic Shifts

When the main reporting verb is in the **past tense** (*said, told*), the verbs in the reported clause shift back one step into the past (**tense backshifting**).

### Subtopic 1: Tense Backshifting Matrix

- **Present Simple $\rightarrow$ Past Simple:** *"I work hard"* $\rightarrow$ *He said that he worked hard.*
- **Present Continuous $\rightarrow$ Past Continuous:** *"I am working"* $\rightarrow$ *He said that he was working.*
- **Past Simple / Present Perfect $\rightarrow$ Past Perfect:** *"I worked/have worked"* $\rightarrow$ *He said that he had worked.*
- **Will $\rightarrow$ Would / Can $\rightarrow$ Could / May $\rightarrow$ Might:** *"I will come"* $\rightarrow$ *He said he would come.*

> **Exception:** Tenses do **not** backshift if the reported statement expresses a universal truth/general fact (*The teacher said, "Earth revolves around the Sun" $\rightarrow$ The teacher said that Earth revolves around the Sun*), or if the reporting verb is in the present tense (*He says...*).

### Subtopic 2: Deictic Adverb and Pronoun Shifts

Perspectives of time, place, and person shift to match the reporting context:

- **Time/Place Shifts:** *now $\rightarrow$ then*, *today $\rightarrow$ that day*, *yesterday $\rightarrow$ the day before / the previous day*, *tomorrow $\rightarrow$ the next day / the following day*, *here $\rightarrow$ there*, *this $\rightarrow$ that*.
- **Reporting Questions:**
  - *Yes/No Questions:* Introduced by *if* or *whether*; word order changes from interrogative to declarative ($\text{Subject} + \text{Verb}$).
    - *"Did you finish?"* $\rightarrow$ *He asked **if I had finished**.*
  - *Wh- Questions:* Retain the $Wh\text{-word}$, but invert to declarative order.
    - *"Where do you live?"* $\rightarrow$ *She asked **where I lived**.*

## Important Classifications / Structures / Processes

| Direct Speech Tense / Type | Indirect Speech Transformation | Direct Example | Indirect Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Present Simple | Past Simple | "I **know** the answer." | He said he **knew** the answer. |
| Present Continuous | Past Continuous | "She **is writing**." | He said she **was writing**. |
| Past Simple | Past Perfect | "We **left** early." | They said they **had left** early. |
| Present Perfect | Past Perfect | "I **have completed** it." | She said she **had completed** it. |
| Interrogative (Yes/No) | *if/whether* + Declarative | "Are you ready?" | He asked **if I was** ready. |
| Imperative / Command | *to* + Bare Infinitive ($V_1$) | "Close the door." | She told me **to close** the door. |

## Important Formulas / Rules / Relationships

Reported Question Inversion Removal Formula:

$$\text{Direct Question}: \text{Reporting Verb} + \text{"} [Wh\text{-word}] + \mathbf{\text{Auxiliary}} + \mathbf{\text{Subject}} + \text{Main Verb?"} \quad (\text{Interrogative Order})$$

$$\Downarrow$$

$$\text{Indirect Question}: \text{Reporting Verb} + [Wh\text{-word} / \text{if} / \text{whether}] + \mathbf{\text{Subject}} + \mathbf{\text{Backshifted Verb}} \quad (\text{Declarative Order})$$

## Common Exam Traps

- **Retaining Interrogative Word Order in Reported Questions:** Failing to invert subject and verb back to declarative structure (e.g., writing *He asked me where was I going* instead of *He asked me where I was going*).
- **Backshifting Universal Scientific Truths:** Erroneously backshifting timeless physical facts (e.g., writing *The instructor explained that water boiled at 100°C* instead of *boils at 100°C*).
- **Confusing "Say" and "Tell" Construction:** Placing a direct personal object after *say* without *to* (e.g., writing *He said me that...* instead of *He told me that...* or *He said to me that...*).

## Quiz

1. Convert to indirect speech: The researcher said, "The preliminary results are promising."
  - [ ] The researcher said that the preliminary results are promising.
  - [x] The researcher said that the preliminary results were promising.
  - [ ] The researcher told that the preliminary results were promising.
  - [ ] The researcher says that the preliminary results had been promising.
   > Past tense reporting verb "said" triggers tense backshifting from Present Simple ("are") to Past Simple ("were").

2. Select the sentence containing the correctly transformed indirect question.
  - [ ] She inquired where had I stored the archival files.
  - [ ] She inquired that where I had stored the archival files.
  - [x] She inquired where I had stored the archival files.
  - [ ] She inquired where did I store the archival files.
   > The reported question drops question inversion ($\text{Auxiliary} + \text{Subject}$) and adopts declarative word order ($\text{Subject} + \text{Verb}$: "where I had stored").

3. What is the correct reported version of: The commander ordered the squad, "Advance immediately!"
  - [ ] The commander ordered the squad that they must advance immediately.
  - [x] The commander ordered the squad to advance immediately.
  - [ ] The commander ordered the squad that advance immediately.
  - [ ] The commander ordered the squad advancing immediately.
   > Direct imperatives/commands convert into infinitival clauses introduced by "to + base verb".
