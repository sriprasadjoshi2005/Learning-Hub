### Section 4: Reading Comprehension and Critical Reasoning

*Passage Excerpt for Questions 8–10:*
> "Empirical studies in cognitive neuroscience demonstrate that deep reading—the array of sophisticated processes that propel comprehension, including inferential reasoning, analogical thought, and critical analysis—is under threat from digital consumption habits. When reading digitally, the human brain tends to employ a rapid, skimming strategy, sampling keywords rather than processing detailed syntactic structures. This shift is not merely behavioral; it reflects neural plasticity. The brain rewires its circuits to favor rapid information gathering at the expense of reflective analytical capacity."

8. What is the primary purpose of the passage?

   - (C) To prove that analog print books are economically superior to digital media

   - (A) The brain increases memory retention for statistical figures.

10. Which statement, if true, would most severely WEAKEN the author's central claim regarding neural plasticity and digital reading?
    - (A) Digital reading devices feature adjustable font sizes and backlighting.

    - (C) Print books remain popular among university literature majors.

## Answer Key & Explanations

1. **(B)** "Having evaluated..." is a dangling modifier in the original sentence. It must modify the actor who evaluated the data ("the lead scientist").
2. **(B)** Disjunctive subjects connected by *neither... nor* follow the Rule of Proximity; the verb agrees with the nearest subject ("research assistants", which is plural $\rightarrow$ *are attending*).
3. **(B)** Mandative verbs (*insisted that...*) require the bare base verb form (*alter*).
4. **(B)** Contrast pivot "Although" signals opposite directions between initial appearances (*promising*) and subsequent discoveries (*severe flaws*).
5. **(C)** "Comply" requires the fixed dependent preposition *with*.
6. **(B)** In academic contexts, to "qualify" a claim means to limit or moderate its scope.
7. **(B)** Sequence **R–P–Q–S**: R introduces the historical context; P details the result ("This rapid expansion..."); Q introduces reforms ("Consequently..."); S elaborates on "These reforms".
8. **(B)** The text focuses on how digital reading habits rewire neural circuits to favor skimming over deep analytical processing.
9. **(B)** Explicitly stated in the final sentence: the brain rewires to favor rapid gathering at the expense of reflective analysis.
10. **(B)** Demonstrating that digital readers retain identical inferential reasoning circuits directly undermines the claim that digital reading rewires the brain to diminish those circuits.

## Common Exam Traps

- **Dangling Modifiers in SC:** Failing to ensure introductory participial phrases touch the correct subject.
- **Proximity Rule Violations:** Forgetting that *neither... nor* matches the verb to the nearest subject.
- **Over-extrapolating in RC:** Selecting extreme or unsupported choices not grounded in the text.
