## title: Organisms and Populations
level: intermediate → advanced

Ecology is the scientific study of interactions among organisms and between organisms and their physical (abiotic) environment. 

## Abiotic Factors and Adaptations

Organisms adapt to abiotic factors (Temperature, Water, Light, Soil) through physiological or behavioral mechanisms:
* **Regulators**: Maintain internal homeostasis (e.g., mammals, birds).
* **Conformers**: Internal environment changes with external conditions (e.g., $99\%$ of animals, plants).
* **Allen's Rule**: Mammals from colder climates have **shorter ears and limbs** to minimize heat loss.

## Population Growth Models

$$
\text{Exponential Growth: } \frac{dN}{dt} = rN \quad \text{and} \quad \text{Logistic Growth: } \frac{dN}{dt} = rN \left( \frac{K - N}{K} \right)
$$

* $N$: Population density.
* $r$: Intrinsic rate of natural increase.
* $K$: Carrying capacity of the environment.
* **Logistic Growth Curve**: Real-world S-shaped (Sigmoid) curve with a Carrying Capacity limit ($K$).

```
 Population (N)
       ^
    K -|--------------------- Carrying Capacity (K)
       |           /--- S-shaped Logistic Curve
       |          /
       |     -----
       +-----------------------> Time (t)
```

## Population Interactions

| Interaction Type | Species A | Species B | Ecological Example |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Mutualism** | $+$ | $+$ | Lichens (Fungus + Alga), Mycorrhizae (Fungus + Plant root) |
| **Competition** | $-$ | $-$ | Abingdon tortoise extinction due to goats on Galapagos |
| **Predation** | $+$ | $-$ | Starfish *Pisaster* maintaining invertebrate diversity |
| **Parasitism** | $+$ | $-$ | *Cuscuta* on hedge plants, Ticks on dogs |
| **Commensalism** | $+$ | $0$ | Orchid growing on a mango branch; Cattle egret and grazing cattle |
| **Amensalism** | $-$ | $0$ | *Penicillium* producing penicillin inhibiting bacteria |

## Common Exam Traps

* **Gause's Competitive Exclusion Principle**: Two closely related species competing for the exact same limited resource cannot coexist indefinitely; the inferior competitor will be eliminated unless **resource partitioning** occurs.
* **Logistic vs Exponential**: Exponential growth occurs when resources are **unlimited** (J-shaped curve); Logistic growth occurs when resources are **limited** (Sigmoid curve).

## Quiz

1. A population of rodents growing in an isolated island ecosystem exhibits a logistic growth pattern. What happens to the net population growth rate ($\frac{dN}{dt}$) when the population size ($N$) reaches the environment's carrying capacity ($K$)?

* \[ \] Growth rate reaches maximum exponential velocity ($rN$).
* \[x\] Growth rate drops to zero ($\frac{dN}{dt} = 0$).
* \[ \] Carrying capacity doubles automatically.
* \[ \] Mortality drops to zero while birth rate surges.

> In logistic growth $\frac{dN}{dt} = rN \left(\frac{K-N}{K}\right)$, when $N = K$, the term $\frac{K-N}{K} = 0$, causing net growth rate $\frac{dN}{dt}$ to equal zero.

2. Mammals adapted to cold sub-polar regions typically possess shorter external extremities (ears, snout, and limbs) than their warm-climate counterparts to conserve body heat. Which ecological principle describes this adaptation?

* \[ \] Gause's Principle
* \[x\] Allen's Rule
* \[ \] Bergmann's Rule
* \[ \] Lindeman's Efficiency Law

> Allen's rule states that endothermic animals from colder climates tend to have shorter limbs and extremities to minimize surface-area-to-volume ratio and reduce heat loss.

3. An epiphyte orchid grows attached to the sturdy branch of a tall mango tree. The orchid gains structural support and light access, while the mango tree experiences neither benefit nor harm. What type of ecological interaction is shown?

* \[ \] Mutualism
* \[ \] Amensalism
* \[x\] Commensalism
* \[ \] Parasitism

> Commensalism is an interaction where one species benefits ($+$) while the other remains completely unaffected ($0$).
