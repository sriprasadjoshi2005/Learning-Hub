## title: Excretory Products and Their Elimination
level: intermediate → advanced

Excretion is the elimination of nitrogenous metabolic wastes (ammonia, urea, uric acid) and the maintenance of salt and water balance (**osmoregulation**).

## Patterns of Nitrogenous Excretion

1. **Ammonotelism**: Excreting highly toxic **Ammonia**; requires massive water for diffusion (e.g., Aquatic invertebrates, bony fishes, tadpoles).
2. **Ureotelism**: Excreting moderately toxic **Urea** synthesized in liver via **Ornithine Cycle** (e.g., Mammals, adult amphibians, marine fishes).
3. **Uricotelism**: Excreting least toxic, insoluble **Uric Acid** as paste/pellets with minimal water loss (e.g., Birds, reptiles, insects, land snails).

## Nephron Structure and Microscopic Anatomy

The **Nephron** is the structural unit of the kidney, consisting of:
* **Renal Corpuscle**: Malpighian body = **Glomerulus** + **Bowman's Capsule**.
* **Renal Tubule**: Proximal Convoluted Tubule (**PCT**) $\rightarrow$ Loop of Henle (Descending & Ascending limbs) $\rightarrow$ Distal Convoluted Tubule (**DCT**) $\rightarrow$ Collecting Duct.

## Physiology of Urine Formation

$$
\text{Urine Formation} = \text{Glomerular Filtration} - \text{Tubular Reabsorption} + \text{Tubular Secretion}
$$

1. **Glomerular Filtration Rate (GFR)**: Volume of filtrate formed by kidneys per minute ($\approx 125\text{ mL/min}$ or $180\text{ L/day}$).
   * Filtration membrane: Glomerular capillary endothelium + Basement membrane + Podocyte slit pores.
2. **Tubular Reabsorption**:
   * **PCT**: Reabsorbs $70-80\%$ of electrolytes and water, and $100\%$ of glucose and amino acids (simple cuboidal brush border epithelium).
   * **Loop of Henle**: Establishes osmotic gradient in inner medulla via **Counter-Current Mechanism** (Vasa Recta + Henle's Loop).
3. **Regulation of Renal Function**:
   * **RAAS Pathway**: Juxtaglomerular (JG) cells release **Renin** when GFR drops $\rightarrow$ Angiotensinogen $\rightarrow$ Angiotensin I $\rightarrow$ Angiotensin II $\rightarrow$ Secretion of **Aldosterone** (absorbs $\mathrm{Na^+}$ and water).
   * **ADH (Vasopressin)**: Released from posterior pituitary; increases water permeability of DCT and collecting duct.

## Common Exam Traps

* **Reabsorption in PCT**: All glucose and amino acids are completely reabsorbed ($100\%$) in the **PCT** via active symport mechanisms under healthy conditions.
* **Counter-Current Multiplier**: Descending limb of Loop of Henle is permeable to **water** but impermeable to electrolytes; Ascending limb is permeable to **electrolytes** but impermeable to water.
