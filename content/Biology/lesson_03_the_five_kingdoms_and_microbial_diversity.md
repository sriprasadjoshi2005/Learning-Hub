## title: The Five Kingdoms and Microbial Diversity
level: foundation

In 1969, **R.H. Whittaker** proposed the **Five Kingdom Classification** system. This system classifies all living organisms into Monera, Protista, Fungi, Plantae, and Animalia based on five main criteria: cell structure, thallus organisation, mode of nutrition, reproduction, and phylogenetic relationships.

## The Five Kingdoms Overview

| Kingdom | Cell Type | Cell Wall | Nuclear Membrane | Mode of Nutrition | Example Organisms |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Monera** | Prokaryotic | Non-cellulosic (Polysaccharide + amino acid) | Absent | Autotrophic (photosynthetic/chemosynthetic) & Heterotrophic (saprophytic/parasitic) | Bacteria, *Cyanobacteria*, Mycoplasma, *Archaea* |
| **Protista** | Eukaryotic | Present in some | Present | Autotrophic & Heterotrophic | Diatoms, Dinoflagellates, Euglenoids, Amoeba, Paramecium |
| **Fungi** | Eukaryotic | Present (composed of **chitin**) | Present | Heterotrophic (Saprophytic / Parasitic / Symbiotic) | Yeast, Mucor, Penicillium, Mushrooms |
| **Plantae** | Eukaryotic | Present (composed of **cellulose**) | Present | Autotrophic (Photosynthetic) | Mosses, Ferns, Gymnosperms, Angiosperms |
| **Animalia** | Eukaryotic | Absent | Present | Heterotrophic (Holozoic / Saprophytic) | Sponges, Insects, Birds, Mammals |

## Sub-Cellular and Non-Cellular Entities

Whittaker's classification system does not include certain non-cellular organisms because they are not truly "living" when outside a host cell:

* **Viruses**: Non-cellular entities consisting of genetic material ($\mathrm{DNA}$ or $\mathrm{RNA}$) enclosed inside a protein coat (**capsid**). They are inert outside a living host.
* **Viroids**: Discovered by T.O. Diener (1971), they are infectious RNA particles **lacking a protein coat** (smaller than viruses; e.g., potato spindle tuber disease).
* **Prions**: Infectious proteinaceous agents causing neurodegenerative disorders (e.g., bovine spongiform encephalopathy or "mad cow disease").
* **Lichens**: Symbiotic associations between algae (**phycobiont**) and fungi (**mycobiont**). The algae prepare food, while the fungus provides shelter and absorbs mineral nutrients. They are sensitive indicators of air pollution ($\mathrm{SO_2}$).

## Archaea

Archaea represent ancient prokaryotic organisms surviving in extreme habitats due to unique cell wall and membrane lipid structures:
* **Halophiles**: Extreme salty areas.
* **Thermoacidophiles**: Hot springs and acidic environments.
* **Methanogens**: Marshy areas and ruminant guts (produce biogas / $\mathrm{CH_4}$).

## Common Exam Traps

* **Excluding Viruses from Kingdoms**: Viruses, viroids, and prions are **not** placed in any of the five kingdoms because they lack cellular structure.
* **Cell wall composition confusion**: Fungal cell walls are made of **chitin**, whereas plant cell walls are made of **cellulose**.
* **Viroid structure**: Assuming viroids contain a capsid. Viroids consist *only* of free low-molecular-weight $\mathrm{RNA}$.
