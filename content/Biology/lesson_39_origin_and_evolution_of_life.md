## title: Origin and Evolution of Life
level: intermediate → advanced

Evolutionary biology studies the history of life forms on Earth. Earth formed $\sim 4.5\text{ billion years ago}$ ($\mathrm{bya}$), and life originated approximately $4.0\text{ bya}$.

## Theories of Origin of Life

1. **Chemical Evolution (Oparin-Haldane Hypothesis)**: First life forms arose from non-living organic molecules ("abiogenesis preceded biogenesis") in a reducing atmosphere ($\mathrm{CH_4, NH_3, H_2O, H_2}$, no free $\mathrm{O_2}$).
2. **Urey-Miller Experiment (1953)**: Recreated early Earth conditions using electric sparks ($800^\circ\mathrm{C}$) in a closed flask containing $\mathrm{CH_4, NH_3, H_2, \text{and } H_2O}$ vapor. Produced **amino acids** (glycine, alanine, aspartic acid), proving chemical evolution.

## Evidence for Biological Evolution

### 1. Homologous vs Analogous Structures

```
                      Evolutionary Anatomical Patterns
                                     |
         -----------------------------------------------------------
         |                                                         |
  Homologous Structures                                    Analogous Structures
(Same origin/anatomy, Different function)                (Different origin, Same function)
  - Indicates **Divergent Evolution**                      - Indicates **Convergent Evolution**
  - Example: Forelimbs of Human, Cheetah,                  - Example: Wings of Butterfly and Bird;
    Whale, and Bat                                           Eye of Octopus and Mammal
```

### 2. Adaptive Radiation
Evolution of different species starting from a common point and radiating to distinct habitats (e.g., **Darwin’s Finches** in Galapagos Islands; Australian marsupials).

### 3. Embryological Evidence
Karl Ernst von Baer disproved Ernst Haeckel’s "Biogenetic Law" (*Ontogeny recapitulates Phylogeny*), showing that embryos never pass through the adult stages of other animals.

## Common Exam Traps

* **Reducing vs Oxidizing Primitive Atmosphere**: Early Earth atmosphere was **reducing** (lacked free $\mathrm{O_2}$). Free oxygen appeared later due to cyanobacterial oxygenic photosynthesis.
* **Homology vs Analogy**: **Homology** implies common ancestry (**divergent evolution**); **Analogy** implies superficial functional similarity adapted to similar selective pressures (**convergent evolution**).

## Quiz

1. Stanley Miller created electric sparks in a sealed laboratory apparatus containing methane, ammonia, hydrogen, and water vapor at $800^\circ\text{C}$. Which organic molecules were synthesized, validating Oparin and Haldane's hypothesis?

* \[ \] Polynucleotides and DNA
* \[x\] Amino acids
* \[ \] Complex fatty acid bilayers
* \[ \] Polysaccharides like cellulose

> Miller's spark-discharge experiment successfully synthesized amino acids (such as glycine and alanine) from inorganic precursors, demonstrating abiogenic organic synthesis.

2. The forelimbs of a human, a cheetah, a whale, and a bat share identical anatomical bone arrangements (humerus, radius, ulna, carpals) despite being adapted for different functional tasks. What evolutionary pattern is demonstrated?

* \[x\] Homologous structures resulting from divergent evolution
* \[ \] Analogous structures resulting from convergent evolution
* \[ \] Vestigial structures resulting from saltation
* \[ \] Atavistic traits resulting from genetic drift

> Homologous structures share a common anatomical origin and basic plan due to shared ancestry, but diverge functionally under different environmental selection pressures.

3. The sweet potato (root modification) and the common Irish potato (stem modification) both function as underground storage organs. How should these structures be classified evolutionarily?

* \[ \] Homologous structures
* \[x\] Analogous structures resulting from convergent evolution
* \[ \] Vestigial organs
* \[ \] Homoplasies due to adaptive radiation

> Sweet potato (root) and Irish potato (stem) have different anatomical origins but perform the same functional role (food storage), making them analogous structures formed by convergent evolution.
