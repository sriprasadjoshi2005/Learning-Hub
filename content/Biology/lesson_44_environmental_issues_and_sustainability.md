## title: Environmental Issues and Sustainability
level: intermediate → advanced

Pollution is any undesirable change in physical, chemical, or biological characteristics of air, land, or water. Sustainable management aims to mitigate human environmental impact.

## Air and Water Pollution Control

* **Electrostatic Precipitator**: Removes over $99\%$ of **particulate matter** from thermal power plant exhaust using high-voltage electrode wires to charge dust particles.
* **Scrubber**: Removes gases like **Sulfur Dioxide ($\mathrm{SO_2}$)** by passing exhaust through a spray of lime or water.
* **Catalytic Converters**: Fitted in automobiles containing expensive metals (**Platinum-Palladium** and **Rhodium**) to convert unburnt hydrocarbons to $\mathrm{CO_2} + \mathrm{H_2O}$, carbon monoxide to $\mathrm{CO_2}$, and nitric oxide to $\mathrm{N_2}$ gas.

## Water Quality Parameters

* **Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD)**: Amount of oxygen required by microbes to oxidize organic matter in water. High $\text{BOD}$ indicates **high organic pollution** and low dissolved oxygen ($\text{DO}$).

```
 Organic Discharge ---> Microbial Proliferation ---> High BOD ---> Dissolved O₂ Drops ---> Fish Mortality
```

* **Eutrophication**: Natural or accelerated (**cultural**) aging of a lake due to nutrient enrichment ($\mathrm{N}$ and $\mathrm{P}$).
* **Biomagnification**: Increase in concentration of non-biodegradable toxic substances (e.g., $\text{DDT}$, Mercury) at successive trophic levels in an aquatic food chain.

## Global Environmental Phenomena

* **Greenhouse Effect & Global Warming**: Trapping of infrared radiation by gases ($\mathrm{CO_2}\ [60\%], \mathrm{CH_4}\ [20\%], \text{CFCs}\ [14\%], \mathrm{N_2O}\ [6\%]$).
* **Ozone Depletion**: Breakdown of stratospheric ozone ("good ozone") by **Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs)** releasing reactive Chlorine atoms. Measured in **Dobson Units (DU)**. Causes "ozone hole" over Antarctica. Protected by the **Montreal Protocol (1987)**.

## Common Exam Traps

* **Good vs Bad Ozone**: "Bad" ozone forms in the lower atmosphere (**troposphere**) and harms plants/animals; "Good" ozone resides in the **stratosphere** and absorbs harmful $\text{UV-B}$ radiation.
* **Unleaded Petrol Requirement**: Vehicles equipped with catalytic converters must use **unleaded petrol** because lead inactivates platinum-rhodium catalysts.

## Quiz

1. Why must motor vehicles fitted with catalytic converters use unleaded petrol exclusively?

* \[ \] Lead causes corrosion of the internal combustion engine cylinders.
* \[x\] Lead inactivates the platinum-palladium and rhodium catalysts inside the converter.
* \[ \] Lead increases carbon monoxide emissions beyond regulatory limits.
* \[ \] Lead reacts with nitrogen oxides to produce toxic smog.

> Lead in petrol acts as a catalyst poison, inactivating the platinum, palladium, and rhodium metals inside catalytic converters.

2. A river receives heavy discharge of untreated domestic sewage. What changes occur in its Biochemical Oxygen Demand ($\text{BOD}$) and Dissolved Oxygen ($\text{DO}$) levels downstream?

* \[ \] $\text{BOD}$ decreases and $\text{DO}$ increases sharply.
* \[x\] $\text{BOD}$ increases sharply while $\text{DO}$ drops, causing fish mortality.
* \[ \] Both $\text{BOD}$ and $\text{DO}$ increase simultaneously.
* \[ \] $\text{BOD}$ and $\text{DO}$ remain completely unaffected.

> Microbes consume dissolved oxygen to decompose organic sewage, causing a sharp increase in $\text{BOD}$ and a rapid decrease in $\text{DO}$.

3. Stratospheric ozone thickness is measured in Dobson Units ($\text{DU}$). What international treaty was signed in 1987 to control the emission of ozone-depleting substances like CFCs?

* \[ \] Kyoto Protocol
* \[x\] Montreal Protocol
* \[ \] Earth Summit (Rio de Janeiro)
* \[ \] Ramsar Convention

> The Montreal Protocol was signed in 1987 (effective 1989) to reduce emissions of Chlorofluorocarbons and protect the stratospheric ozone layer.
