## Quiz

1. A cell biologist isolates a mutant strain of *E. coli* that synthesizes functional $\text{RNA}$ Polymerase but fails to initiate transcription at promoter regions specifically under high-temperature stress conditions. Which transcription factor subunit is mutated?

* \[ \] Alpha ($\alpha$) subunit
* \[ \] Beta ($\beta$) subunit
* \[x\] Sigma ($\sigma$) factor
* \[ \] Rho ($\rho$) termination factor

> In prokaryotes, the Sigma ($\sigma$) factor binds to core $\text{RNA}$ Polymerase to form the holoenzyme, conferring specificity for promoter recognition and transcription initiation.

2. A sample of double-stranded $\text{DNA}$ contains $3.2 \times 10^6$ base pairs. Calculate the total physical length of this $\text{DNA}$ molecule and the total number of hydrogen bonds if Cytosine accounts for $30\%$ of all bases.

* \[ \] Length $= 0.54\text{ mm}$; Hydrogen bonds $= 6.4 \times 10^6$
* \[x\] Length $= 1.088\text{ mm}$; Hydrogen bonds $= 8.32 \times 10^6$
* \[ \] Length $= 2.176\text{ mm}$; Hydrogen bonds $= 9.60 \times 10^6$
* \[ \] Length $= 1.088\text{ cm}$; Hydrogen bonds $= 4.16 \times 10^6$

> Length $= 3.2 \times 10^6 \times 0.34 \times 10^{-9}\text{ m} = 1.088 \times 10^{-3}\text{ m} = 1.088\text{ mm}$. Total bases $= 6.4 \times 10^6$. If $\% \text{C} = 30\%$, then $\% \text{G} = 30\%$, $\% \text{A} = 20\%$, $\% \text{T} = 20\%$. Base pairs: $\text{G-C} = 1.92 \times 10^6$ pairs ($\times 3\text{ H-bonds} = 5.76 \times 10^6$), $\text{A-T} = 1.28 \times 10^6$ pairs ($\times 2\text{ H-bonds} = 2.56 \times 10^6$). Total H-bonds $= 5.76 \times 10^6 + 2.56 \times 10^6 = 8.32 \times 10^6$.

3. In a study of an island ecosystem, ecologists determine that the rate of population increase ($\frac{dN}{dt}$) is maximum when the population size ($N$) is equal to exactly half of the carrying capacity ($\frac{K}{2}$). Which growth equation governs this population, and what is this point called in population ecology?

* \[ \] Exponential growth; Inflection point
* \[x\] Logistic growth ($ \frac{dN}{dt} = rN \left[ \frac{K-N}{K} \right] $); Inflection point
* \[ \] Geometric growth; Carrying limit
* \[ \] Haplontic growth; Equilibrium point

> In logistic population growth ($\frac{dN}{dt} = rN\left[\frac{K-N}{K}\right]$), maximum growth velocity occurs at $N = \frac{K}{2}$, which is termed the point of inflection.
