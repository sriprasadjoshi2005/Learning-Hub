## title: Human Reproduction
level: intermediate → advanced

Human reproduction involves internal fertilization and viviparity. The process includes gametogenesis, fertilization, implantation, gestation, and parturition under complex hormonal control.

## Male and Female Reproductive Systems

* **Male Anatomy**: Testes located outside the abdominal cavity in the **scrotum** (maintains temperature $2-2.5^\circ\mathrm{C}$ lower than body core for spermatogenesis).
  * *Spermatogenesis*: Occurs in **seminiferous tubules**. **Sertoli cells** nourish developing sperm; **Leydig cells** produce **Testosterone** under $\mathrm{LH}$ stimulation.
* **Female Anatomy**: Ovaries, Fallopian tubes (Oviducts), Uterus, Cervix, Vagina.
  * *Oogenesis*: Initiated during embryonic development; halted at **Prophase I** (Diplotene stage) until puberty. Completed only upon fertilization by a sperm.

## Gametogenesis Comparison

| Parameter | Spermatogenesis | Oogenesis |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Site** | Seminiferous tubules of testes | Ovaries |
| **Product** | 4 equal functional flagellated sperm ($n$) | 1 large ovum ($n$) + 2-3 degenerate polar bodies |
| **Continuity** | Continuous process from puberty to old age | Discontinuous; pauses at Prophase I, halts at **Menopause** (~50 years) |

## The Menstrual Cycle (Average Duration: 28 Days)

```
 Days 1-5: Menstrual Phase  --->  Days 6-13: Follicular/Proliferative Phase  --->  Day 14: Ovulation  --->  Days 15-28: Luteal/Secretory Phase
 (Endometrial shedding)           (FSH stimulates follicular growth;             (LH Surge triggers        (Corpus luteum secretes Progesterone;
                                  Estrogen rises)                                ovum release)             prepares uterus for implantation)
```

## Fertilization and Early Embryogenesis

$$
\text{Fertilization (Ampulla of Oviduct)} \longrightarrow \text{Zygote } (2n) \longrightarrow \text{Cleavage} \longrightarrow \text{Morula} \longrightarrow \text{Blastocyst} \xrightarrow[\text{Day 7}]{\text{Implantation}} \text{Uterine Endometrium}
$$

* **Capacitation**: Functional maturation of sperm inside the female reproductive tract enabling acrosome reaction.
* **Site of Fertilization**: **Ampullary region** of the Fallopian tube.
* **Implantation**: The **Blastocyst** (outer **trophoblast** layer + inner cell mass) embeds into the endometrium.

## Common Exam Traps

* **Exact Fertilization Site**: Fertilization takes place specifically in the **ampulla** of the Fallopian tube.
* **Corpus Luteum Maintenance**: If fertilization occurs, **Human Chorionic Gonadotropin (hCG)** produced by the trophoblast maintains the **corpus luteum** to continue secreting progesterone.

## Quiz

* \[ \] Fimbriae of infundibulum

> Fertilization naturally occurs within the ampullary region of the Fallopian tube (oviduct).

2. What endocrine event triggers the rupture of the mature Graafian follicle and subsequent release of a secondary oocyte (ovulation) around Day 14 of the human menstrual cycle?

* \[ \] Sharp decline in progesterone levels
* \[x\] Rapid mid-cycle peak surge of Luteinizing Hormone ($\text{LH}$)
* \[ \] Continuous elevation of Follicle-Stimulating Hormone ($\text{FSH}$) only
* \[ \] Complete withdrawal of estrogen

> A sudden rapid rise in Luteinizing Hormone ($\text{LH}$ surge) around the middle of the 28-day cycle triggers the rupture of the Graafian follicle, releasing the secondary oocyte.

3. Following successful implantation, which embryonic tissue layer of the blastocyst secretes Human Chorionic Gonadotropin ($\text{hCG}$) to prevent corpus luteum degeneration?

* \[ \] Inner cell mass
* \[x\] Trophoblast
* \[ \] Endometrial stroma
* \[ \] Zona pellucida

> The outer trophoblast layer of the implanting blastocyst secretes $\text{hCG}$, which keeps the corpus luteum active to maintain high progesterone production during early pregnancy.
