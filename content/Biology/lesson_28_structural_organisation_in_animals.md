## title: Structural Organisation in Animals
level: intermediate

Animal tissues are groups of similar cells performing a specific collective function. Animals possess four basic tissue types: **Epithelial**, **Connective**, **Muscular**, and **Neural** tissues.

## Major Animal Tissue Types

| Tissue Class | Key Characteristics | Sub-types | Key Functions / Locations |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Epithelial** | Tightly packed cells with minimal intercellular matrix; free surface facing fluid or environment | Simple (Squamous, Cuboidal, Columnar, Ciliated) & Compound | Protection, absorption, secretion; alveolar lining, kidney tubules, skin |
| **Connective** | Most abundant; cells secreted in an extracellular matrix (except blood) | Loose (Areolar, Adipose), Dense (Tendons, Ligaments), Specialized (Cartilage, Bone, Blood) | Structural support, binding, transport; tendons join muscle-to-bone, ligaments join bone-to-bone |
| **Muscular** | Elongated contractile fibres containing myofilaments (actin and myosin) | Skeletal (Striated, Voluntary), Smooth (Unstriated, Involuntary), Cardiac (Striated, Involuntary) | Movement, locomotion, blood pumping; intercalated discs present in cardiac muscle |
| **Neural** | Specialized excitable cells (Neurons) supported by Neuroglia | Neurons and Neuroglial cells | Signal transmission and coordination; brain, spinal cord, nerves |

## Cell Junctions in Epithelial Tissues

1. **Tight Junctions**: Prevent leakage of substances across tissues.
2. **Adhering Junctions**: Perform cementing to keep neighboring cells together.
3. **Gap Junctions**: Facilitate intercellular communication by connecting cytoplasm of adjoining cells for rapid transfer of ions and small molecules.

## Common Exam Traps

* **Tendon vs Ligament**: **Tendons** connect Skeletal Muscle to Bone; **Ligaments** connect Bone to Bone.
* **Intercalated Discs**: Intercalated discs containing gap junctions are a unique diagnostic feature of **Cardiac Muscle** only.

## Quiz

1. Histological examination of human tissue reveals multinucleated, cylindrical, striated cells under conscious voluntary control. What type of tissue is being observed?

* \[ \] Smooth muscle tissue
* \[x\] Skeletal muscle tissue
* \[ \] Cardiac muscle tissue
* \[ \] Dense irregular connective tissue

> Skeletal muscle tissue consists of long, cylindrical, striated multinucleated fibres operating under voluntary motor control.

2. Which intercellular epithelial junction functions specifically to prevent fluid from leaking across a tissue barrier into surrounding intercellular spaces?

* \[x\] Tight junction
* \[ \] Adhering junction
* \[ \] Gap junction
* \[ \] Desmosome

> Tight junctions form water-tight seals between adjacent epithelial cells, preventing solutes and liquids from leaking across the epithelial layer.

3. Which specialized connective tissue structure connects skeletal muscles directly to bones?

* \[ \] Ligament
* \[x\] Tendon
* \[ \] Cartilage
* \[ \] Areolar tissue

> Tendons are dense regular connective tissues composed of parallel collagen bundles that attach skeletal muscles to bones. (Ligaments attach bone to bone).
