# Biology syllabus plan (50 lessons, foundation to university entrance-exam level)

Phase 1 : Biological foundations 1–8: Meaning of life, characteristics of life, biological classification, five kingdoms, plant and animal diversity, chemical foundations, biomolecules, and levels of biological organisation.

Phase 2 : Cell biology and genetics 9–17: Cell theory, cell structure and organelles, transport mechanisms, cell division (mitosis and meiosis), Mendelian genetics, chromosomal inheritance, molecular basis of genetics, gene regulation, and mutations.

Phase 3 : Plant biology 18–27: Plant morphology, anatomy, water relations and transport, mineral nutrition, photosynthesis fundamentals and advanced mechanisms, plant respiration, growth, plant hormones, and reproduction in flowering plants.

Phase 4 : Human and animal biology 28–38: Animal tissues, human digestion, respiration, body fluids and circulation, excretion, locomotion, neural control, chemical coordination, human reproduction, reproductive health, and human health/diseases.

Phase 5 : Ecology and evolution 39–44: Origin and evolution of life, evolutionary mechanisms and population genetics, organism-population interactions, ecosystem structure and function, biodiversity conservation, and environmental issues.

Phase 6 : Biotechnology and examination mastery 45–50: Biotechnology principles and tools, applications in medicine and agriculture, advanced biological problem-solving, diagram and experiment analysis, complete revision, and a full-length entrance examination mock test.

The sequence begins with fundamental biological concepts and builds toward problems commonly encountered in board, state-level entrance and competitive university entrance examinations (such as NEET). Check the latest official syllabus for the examination being attempted; this learning plan is a broad study pathway, not an official examination syllabus.
