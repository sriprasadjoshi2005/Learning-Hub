## title: Cell Theory and Cell Structure
level: foundation

Cells are the structural and functional units of all living organisms. Cytology and cell biology focus on understanding how prokaryotic and eukaryotic cell architectures support metabolic processes.

## History and Postulates of Cell Theory

1. **Matthias Schleiden (1838)**: Concluded that all plants are composed of cells.
2. **Theodor Schwann (1839)**: Concluded that all animals are made of cells and identified the thin outer boundary (plasma membrane).
3. **Rudolf Virchow (1855)**: Expanded cell theory by adding the principle ***"Omnis cellula-e cellula"*** (All living cells arise from pre-existing cells).

### Modern Cell Theory Postulates
* All living organisms are composed of one or more cells and cell products.
* The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.
* All cells arise from pre-existing cells through cell division.

## Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic Cell Comparison

| Feature | Prokaryotic Cell | Eukaryotic Cell |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Nuclear Structure** | No true nucleus; genetic material resides in an un-enclosed **nucleoid** region | True nucleus surrounded by double-membrane **nuclear envelope** |
| **Genetic Material** | Circular double-stranded $\mathrm{DNA}$; lacking histones | Linear double-stranded $\mathrm{DNA}$; complexed with **histone proteins** |
| **Membrane-bound Organelles** | Absent (No mitochondria, ER, Golgi, etc.) | Present (Mitochondria, ER, Golgi, Lysosomes, etc.) |
| **Ribosomes** | $\mathrm{70S}$ (Subunits: $\mathrm{50S} + \mathrm{30S}$) | $\mathrm{80S}$ (Cytoplasmic; Subunits: $\mathrm{60S} + \mathrm{40S}$) |
| **Cell Wall** | Present in most; composed of **peptidoglycan** (murein) | Present in plants (cellulose) and fungi (chitin); absent in animals |

## Bacterial Cell Envelope Modifications

Prokaryotes often possess a complex three-layered cell envelope:

$$
\text{Outer Glycocalyx (Capsule/Slime Layer)} \longrightarrow \text{Cell Wall} \longrightarrow \text{Plasma Membrane}
$$

* **Mesosomes**: Infoldings of the prokaryotic plasma membrane that function in cellular respiration, cell wall formation, and $\mathrm{DNA}$ replication (analogous to eukaryotic mitochondria).

## Common Exam Traps

* **Histone Association**: Prokaryotic genomic $\mathrm{DNA}$ is *naked* (not associated with eukaryotic histone proteins), though it is packaged by polyamines and non-histone architectural proteins.
* **Exceptions to Cell Theory**: Viruses, viroids, and red blood cells (erythrocytes lacking nuclei at maturity) represent notable exceptions or modifications to classic cell theory principles.
