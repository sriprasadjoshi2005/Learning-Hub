## title: Human Health, Immunity and Disease
level: intermediate → advanced

Health is a state of complete physical, mental, and social well-being. Pathogens breach body defenses to cause disease, while the immune system defends against non-self entities.

## Types of Immunity

```
                               Immune System
                                     |
         -----------------------------------------------------------
         |                                                         |
  Innate Immunity                                          Acquired Immunity
(Non-specific; Present from birth)                       (Pathogen-specific; Memory-based)
  - Physical (Skin, Mucosa)                                - Humoral (B-cells -> Antibodies)
  - Physiological (Acid, Lysozyme)                         - Cell-Mediated (T-cells)
  - Cellular (Neutrophils, Macrophages)
  - Cytokine (Interferons)
```

### Antibody Structure ($\mathrm{H_2L_2}$)
An antibody molecule consists of two heavy chains ($\mathrm{H}$) and two light chains ($\mathrm{L}$) held by disulfide bonds:
* **$\mathrm{IgG}$**: Most abundant; crosses placenta to protect fetus.
* **$\mathrm{IgA}$**: Present in secretions (colostrum/breast milk, saliva, tears).
* **$\mathrm{IgE}$**: Mediates allergic responses and histamine release from mast cells.
* **$\mathrm{IgM}$**: First antibody produced during primary immune response.

## Major Human Pathogens and Diseases

| Disease | Pathogen Type & Name | Vector / Transmission | Key Diagnostic Test / Symptoms |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Typhoid** | Bacteria (*Salmonella typhi*) | Contaminated food and water | **Widal Test**; high sustained fever, intestinal perforation |
| **Pneumonia** | Bacteria (*Streptococcus pneumoniae*) | Droplet inhalation | Fluid accumulation in alveoli; gray/blue fingernails |
| **Malaria** | Protozoan (*Plasmodium falciparum/vivax*) | Female *Anopheles* mosquito | Chills/fever every 3-4 days due to **haemozoin** release |
| **Amoebiasis** | Protozoan (*Entamoeba histolytica*) | Houseflies (mechanical carrier) | Stool with excess mucus and blood clots |
| **Ringworm** | Fungi (*Microsporum*, *Epidermophyton*) | Towels, clothes, soil | Dry, scaly lesions on skin and nails |

## Cancer and AIDS

* **AIDS**: Caused by **Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV)** (Retrovirus). Destroys **Helper T-cells** ($\mathrm{CD4^+}$), crippling cell-mediated immunity. Diagnosed using **ELISA**.
* **Cancer**: Uncontrolled cell proliferation caused by transformation of normal cells into neoplastic cells. Loss of **contact inhibition**. Metastasis is the most dangerous property of malignant tumors.

## Common Exam Traps

* **Malaria Toxin**: The recurring fever and chills in malaria are caused by the release of the toxic substance **haemozoin** when red blood cells rupture.
* **Cell-Mediated Immunity**: Graft rejection during organ transplantation is driven by **Cell-Mediated Immunity (T-lymphocytes)**, not Humoral Immunity.
