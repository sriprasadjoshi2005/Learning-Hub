## title: Respiration in Plants
level: intermediate → advanced

Cellular respiration is a catabolic oxidation process where complex organic molecules (primarily glucose) are broken down to release metabolic energy stored as $\mathrm{ATP}$.

## Stages of Aerobic Respiration

$$
\mathrm{C_6H_{12}O_6 + 6O_2} \longrightarrow \mathrm{6CO_2 + 6H_2O + Energy\ (36-38\ ATP)}
$$

```
 Glucose (6C)
     |
     v [Glycolysis - Cytosol]
 2 Pyruvate (3C) + 2 ATP + 2 NADH
     |
     v [Link Reaction - Mitochondrial Matrix]
 2 Acetyl-CoA (2C) + 2 CO₂ + 2 NADH
     |
     v [Krebs Cycle - Mitochondrial Matrix]
 4 CO₂ + 2 ATP + 6 NADH + 2 FADH₂
     |
     v [ETC & Oxidative Phosphorylation - Inner Mitochondrial Membrane]
 Total Yield: ~36 to 38 ATP
```

## 1. Glycolysis (EMP Pathway)
Occurs in the **cytosol** of all living cells (aerobic and anaerobic):
* Glucose ($6\text{C}$) is converted into $2\text{ molecules}$ of Pyruvic acid ($3\text{C}$).
* **Net Yield**: $2\mathrm{\ ATP} + 2\mathrm{\ NADH}$. (No $\mathrm{CO_2}$ released; independent of $\mathrm{O_2}$).

## 2. Anaerobic Respiration (Fermentation)
In the absence of $\mathrm{O_2}$, pyruvate is processed in the cytoplasm to regenerate $\mathrm{NAD^+}$:
* **Alcoholic Fermentation**: Pyruvate $\rightarrow$ Acetaldehyde $\rightarrow$ Ethanol + $\mathrm{CO_2}$ (Yeast).
* **Lactic Acid Fermentation**: Pyruvate $\rightarrow$ Lactic Acid (Muscle cells, *Lactobacillus*).

## 3. Krebs Cycle (Tricarboxylic Acid / TCA Cycle)
Occurs in the **mitochondrial matrix**:
* Primary acceptor: Oxaloacetic acid ($\mathrm{OAA}$, $4\text{C}$) combines with Acetyl-CoA ($2\text{C}$) to form **Citric Acid** ($6\text{C}$).
* Yield per glucose ($2\text{ turns}$): $2\mathrm{\ GTP/ATP} + 6\mathrm{\ NADH} + 2\mathrm{\ FADH_2} + 4\mathrm{CO_2}$.

## 4. Electron Transport Chain (ETC) & Complex Summary
Located on the **inner mitochondrial membrane**:
* $\mathrm{1\ NADH} \rightarrow 3\mathrm{\ ATP}$ (or $2.5\mathrm{\ ATP}$).
* $\mathrm{1\ FADH_2} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{\ ATP}$ (or $1.5\mathrm{\ ATP}$).
* Final electron acceptor: **Oxygen** ($\mathrm{O_2}$), which combines with protons to form $\mathrm{H_2O}$.

## Respiratory Quotient (RQ)

$$
\text{RQ} = \frac{\text{Volume of } \mathrm{CO_2} \text{ evolved}}{\text{Volume of } \mathrm{O_2} \text{ consumed}}
$$

* Carbohydrates: $\text{RQ} = 1.0$
* Fats/Lipids (e.g., Tripalmitin): $\text{RQ} \approx 0.7$
* Proteins: $\text{RQ} \approx 0.9$
* Organic Acids (e.g., Malic acid): $\text{RQ} > 1.0$

## Common Exam Traps

* **Glycolysis Location & Oxygen Requirement**: Glycolysis occurs in the **cytoplasm**, not the mitochondria, and does **not** consume oxygen gas.
* **Substrate-Level Phosphorylation**: Direct synthesis of $\mathrm{ATP/GTP}$ occurs in Glycolysis (PEP to Pyruvate) and Krebs cycle (Succinyl-CoA to Succinate), independent of the electron transport chain.

* \[ \] $4\ \text{ATP}$ and $2\ \text{NADH}$
* \[x\] $2\ \text{ATP}$ and $2\ \text{NADH}$
* \[ \] $2\ \text{ATP}$ and $4\ \text{NADH}$
* \[ \] $36\ \text{ATP}$ and $0\ \text{NADH}$

> Glycolysis consumes $2\text{ ATP}$ in its preparatory phase and yields $4\text{ ATP}$ and $2\text{ NADH}$ in its payoff phase, giving a net yield of $2\text{ ATP}$ and $2\text{ NADH}$.

* \[ \] $> 1.3$

> Fats contain less oxygen relative to carbon and hydrogen than carbohydrates, requiring more $\text{O}_2$ for complete oxidation. The $\text{RQ}$ for tripalmitin is $0.7$.
