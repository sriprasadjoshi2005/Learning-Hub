## title: Ecosystem Structure and Function
level: intermediate → advanced

An ecosystem is a functional unit of nature where living organisms interact among themselves and with their surrounding physical environment.

## Core Functions of Ecosystems

1. **Productivity**: Rate of biomass production.
   * **Gross Primary Productivity (GPP)**: Total rate of photosynthetic organic matter production.
   * **Net Primary Productivity (NPP)**: Energy remaining after respiratory losses ($\mathrm{R}$).

$$
\mathrm{NPP = GPP - R}
$$

2. **Decomposition**: Breakdown of complex organic matter (detritus) into inorganic nutrients.
   * Stages: Fragmentation $\rightarrow$ Leaching $\rightarrow$ Catabolism $\rightarrow$ Humification $\rightarrow$ Mineralization.
3. **Energy Flow**: Unidirectional movement of energy from producers to consumers.
   * **Lindeman’s $10\%$ Law**: Only $10\%$ of energy is transferred to each successive trophic level; $90\%$ is lost as heat during respiration.

## Primary vs Secondary Ecological Succession

* **Primary Succession**: Occurs on newly exposed bare surfaces lacking soil or organic matter (e.g., bare rock, newly cooled lava). Pioneer species on bare rock are **Lichens**.
* **Secondary Succession**: Occurs in areas where natural vegetation was destroyed but soil remains intact (e.g., abandoned farmland, burned forest). Proceeds **faster** than primary succession.

## Common Exam Traps

* **Pyramid of Energy Never Inverts**: The Pyramid of Energy is **always upright** without exception because energy is lost as heat at every trophic step according to the 2nd Law of Thermodynamics.
* **Aquatic Biomass Inversion**: The Pyramid of Biomass in an aquatic ecosystem is **inverted** because the standing crop of phytoplankton is small relative to larger, long-lived fishes.

## Quiz

1. In a terrestrial forest ecosystem, the Gross Primary Productivity ($\text{GPP}$) is measured at $20,000\text{ kcal/m}^2/\text{yr}$, and autotrophic respiration losses ($\text{R}$) account for $8,000\text{ kcal/m}^2/\text{yr}$. What is the Net Primary Productivity ($\text{NPP}$) available to primary consumers?

* \[ \] $28,000\text{ kcal/m}^2/\text{yr}$
* \[x\] $12,000\text{ kcal/m}^2/\text{yr}$
* \[ \] $16,000\text{ kcal/m}^2/\text{yr}$
* \[ \] $2,000\text{ kcal/m}^2/\text{yr}$

> Net Primary Productivity $\text{NPP} = \text{GPP} - \text{R} = 20,000 - 8,000 = 12,000\text{ kcal/m}^2/\text{yr}$.

2. Which ecological pyramid construction can **never** exist in an inverted orientation in any natural ecosystem?

* \[ \] Pyramid of Numbers in a forest

* \[ \] Pyramid of Biomass in a grassland

> The Pyramid of Energy is universally upright because energy is lost as heat at every trophic transition ($10\%$ rule), making an inverted energy pyramid physically impossible.
