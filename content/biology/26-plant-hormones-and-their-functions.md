## title: Plant Hormones and Their Functions
level: intermediate → advanced

Plant Growth Regulators (PGRs) or plant hormones are small organic molecules that control plant physiological processes, growth responses, and developmental patterns.

## Major Plant Growth Regulator Classes

Plant hormones are grouped into **Growth Promoters** (Auxins, Gibberellins, Cytokinins) and **Growth Inhibitors** (Abscisic Acid, Ethylene).

| Hormone Class | Primary Biosynthetic Precursor | Major Physiological Roles | Key Practical / Agricultural Applications |
| ----- | ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Auxins** (e.g., $\text{IAA}$, $\text{NAA}$, $2,4\text{-D}$) | Tryptophan | Apical dominance, cell elongation, root initiation | $2,4\text{-D}$ used as a selective herbicide against broad-leaved dicot weeds; rooting powder |
| **Gibberellins** (e.g., $\text{GA}_3$) | Acetyl-$\text{CoA}$ / Terpenes | Stem bolting (internode elongation), breakdown of seed dormancy | Increasing stalk length of grapes; malting in brewing industry; delays senescence |
| **Cytokinins** (e.g., Zeatin, Kinetin) | Adenine (Purine derivative) | Promotes cell division, delays leaf senescence (**Richmond-Lang Effect**), overcomes apical dominance | Tissue culture micropropagation; promoting shoot initiation |
| **Abscisic Acid (ABA)** | Carotenoids | Stomatal closure under water stress, promotes seed dormancy | Known as the **Stress Hormone**; inhibits seed germination |
| **Ethylene** ($\mathrm{C_2H_4}$) | Methionine | Triple response, fruit ripening, promotes abscission | Ethephon used to accelerate fruit ripening (tomatoes, apples) and induce flowering in pineapple |

## Inter-Hormonal Interactions

* **Apical Dominance**: Auxin synthesized at the shoot apex inhibits lateral axillary bud growth. Removing the shoot tip (decapitation) or applying **Cytokinin** counteracts apical dominance, promoting lateral branching.
* **Auxin-to-Cytokinin Ratio in Tissue Culture**:
  * High Auxin : Low Cytokinin $\rightarrow$ Root differentiation (**Rhizogenesis**).
  * Low Auxin : High Cytokinin $\rightarrow$ Shoot differentiation (**Caulogenesis**).
  * Equal Auxin : Cytokinin $\rightarrow$ Undifferentiated cell mass (**Callus**).

## Common Exam Traps

* **Gaseous Hormone Unique Status**: **Ethylene** is the **only** naturally occurring gaseous plant growth regulator.
* **$2,4\text{-D}$ Selectivity**: $2,4\text{-D}$ selectively kills broad-leaved **dicotyledonous** weeds, leaving monocotyledonous cereal crops undamaged.

## Quiz

1. A plant tissue culture scientist adjusts the nutrient medium ratio to contain a high concentration of Cytokinin relative to Auxin. What organ development will be induced from the callus?

* \[ \] Root differentiation
* \[x\] Shoot differentiation
* \[ \] Seed coat formation
* \[ \] Fruit ripening

> A high Cytokinin-to-Auxin ratio induces shoot initiation (caulogenesis), whereas a high Auxin-to-Cytokinin ratio promotes root initiation (rhizogenesis).

2. During severe drought, which plant growth regulator accumulates rapidly in leaves to trigger protective stomatal closure and reduce transpiration water loss?

* \[ \] Gibberellin ($\text{GA}_3$)
* \[ \] Indole-3-acetic acid ($\text{IAA}$)
* \[x\] Abscisic Acid ($\text{ABA}$)
* \[ \] Ethylene

> Abscisic Acid ($\text{ABA}$) is known as the stress hormone. Under water deficit conditions, it causes guard cells to lose turgor, closing stomata to conserve water.

3. The phenomenon of "bolting"—a rapid stem and internode elongation immediately prior to flowering in rosette plants such as cabbage and beet—is induced by applying:

* \[ \] Auxin
* \[x\] Gibberellin
* \[ \] Cytokinin
* \[ \] Abscisic Acid

> Gibberellins promote internodal cell elongation, inducing bolting (rapid stem extension) in rosette plants like cabbage before flowering.
