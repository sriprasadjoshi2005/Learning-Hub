* **Monera vs Protista**: Monera = Prokaryotic; Protista = Unicellular Eukaryotic.
* **Prokaryotic Ribosome**: $\mathrm{70S}$ ($\mathrm{50S} + \mathrm{30S}$); Eukaryotic Cytoplasmic Ribosome: $\mathrm{80S}$ ($\mathrm{60S} + \mathrm{40S}$).

## Assertion-Reasoning Rules

* **Option A**: Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are True, and R is the correct explanation of A.
* **Option B**: Both Assertion (A) and Reason (R) are True, but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.
* **Option C**: Assertion (A) is True, but Reason (R) is False.
* **Option D**: Assertion (A) is False, but Reason (R) is True.

## Common Exam Traps

* **Assertion-Reasoning Fallacy**: Do not automatically mark Option A just because both statements are true. Verify that Reason directly explains the "Why" behind Assertion.
* **Matching Question Column Audit**: Always cross-check at least two known pairs in Match-the-Following questions to avoid elimination traps.

## Quiz

1. **Assertion (A)**: Photorespiration does not occur in $\text{C}_4$ photosynthetic plants like maize.
   **Reason (R)**: $\text{C}_4$ plants maintain a high concentration of $\text{CO}_2$ around the $\text{RuBisCO}$ enzyme in bundle sheath cells via the Hatch-Slack pathway.

* \[x\] Both (A) and (R) are true, and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
* \[ \] Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A).
* \[ \] (A) is true, but (R) is false.
* \[ \] (A) is false, but (R) is true.

> $\text{C}_4$ plants prevent photorespiration because $\text{PEP}$ carboxylase fixes $\text{CO}_2$ in mesophyll cells and transports it as $\text{C}_4$ acids to bundle sheath cells. Decarboxylation concentrates $\text{CO}_2$ around $\text{RuBisCO}$, favoring its carboxylase activity over oxygenase activity.
