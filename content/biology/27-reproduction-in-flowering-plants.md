## title: Reproduction in Flowering Plants
level: intermediate → advanced

Sexual reproduction in angiosperms involves microsporogenesis, megasporogenesis, double fertilization, and seed development within specialized floral structures.

## Male and Female Gametophytes

* **Microsporogenesis**: Development of pollen grains (male gametophytes) in microsporangia (anthers).
  * Microspore mother cell ($2n$) $\xrightarrow{\text{Meiosis}} 4$ microspores ($n$).
  * Mature pollen contains a **Vegetative cell** (large, food reserve) and a **Generative cell** (divides mitotically into 2 male gametes).
* **Megasporogenesis**: Development of embryo sac (female gametophyte) inside the ovule.
  * Megaspore mother cell ($2n$) $\xrightarrow{\text{Meiosis}} 4$ megaspores ($n$) $\rightarrow 3$ degenerate, $1$ functional megaspore develops.
  * **Polygonum Type Embryo Sac**: 7-celled, 8-nucleate structure containing 3 antipodals, 2 synergids with filiform apparatus, 1 egg cell, and 1 central cell with 2 polar nuclei.

## Double Fertilization

Unique to angiosperms, double fertilization consists of two fusion events:

1. **Syngamy**: Male gamete ($n$) + Egg cell ($n$) $\longrightarrow$ **Zygote** ($2n$).
2. **Triple Fusion**: Male gamete ($n$) + 2 Polar nuclei ($n+n$) $\longrightarrow$ **Primary Endosperm Nucleus (PEN)** ($3n$).

$$
\text{Zygote } (2n) \rightarrow \text{Embryo} \quad \text{and} \quad \text{PEN } (3n) \rightarrow \text{Endosperm (Nutritive Tissue)}
$$

## Post-Fertilization Structures

| Pre-Fertilization Structure | Post-Fertilization Product |
| ----- | ----- |
| Ovule | Seed |
| Ovary | Fruit |
| Ovary Wall | Pericarp |
| Integuments (Outer / Inner) | Seed Coat (Testa / Tegmen) |
| Primary Endosperm Nucleus | Endosperm ($3n$) |

## Common Exam Traps

* **Endosperm Ploidy**: Angiosperm endosperm is **triploid** ($3n$), whereas Gymnosperm endosperm is **haploid** ($n$) because it forms before fertilization.
* **Filiform Apparatus Function**: The filiform apparatus located in synergids guides the entry of the pollen tube into the embryo sac.
