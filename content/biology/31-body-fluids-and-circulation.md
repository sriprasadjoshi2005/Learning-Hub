## title: Body Fluids and Circulation
level: intermediate → advanced

Multicellular animals require circulatory systems to transport oxygen, nutrients, hormones, and metabolic wastes. The human cardiovascular system comprises blood, blood vessels, and a four-chambered myogenic heart.

## Blood Composition and Formed Elements

Blood is a fluid connective tissue consisting of $55\%$ **Plasma** and $45\%$ **Formed Elements**:

1. **Erythrocytes (RBCs)**: Biconcave, non-nucleated cells containing hemoglobin ($12-16\text{ g/dL}$); lifespan of $120\text{ days}$.
2. **Leukocytes (WBCs)**: Nucleated immune cells:
   * *Granulocytes*: Neutrophils ($60-65\%$, phagocytic), Eosinophils ($2-3\%$, allergic response), Basophils ($0.5-1\%$, secrete histamine/heparin).
   * *Agranulocytes*: Lymphocytes ($20-25\%$, B and T cell immunity), Monocytes ($6-8\%$, phagocytic tissue macrophages).
3. **Thrombocytes (Platelets)**: Cell fragments produced by **megakaryocytes** in bone marrow; essential for blood clotting.

## Human Cardiac Cycle and Conductive System

$$
\text{SA Node (Pacemaker)} \xrightarrow{} \text{AV Node} \xrightarrow{} \text{Bundle of His} \xrightarrow{} \text{Purkinje Fibres} \xrightarrow{} \text{Ventricular Contraction}
$$

* **Sinoatrial (SA) Node**: Generates spontaneous action potentials ($70-75\text{ beats/min}$), initiating the heartbeat without neural stimulation (**Myogenic Heart**).

### Cardiac Cycle Phases (Duration: $0.8\text{ seconds}$)
1. **Joint Diastole** ($0.4\text{ s}$): All chambers relaxed; AV valves open, blood flows from atrium to ventricles.
2. **Atrial Systole** ($0.1\text{ s}$): Atria contract, increasing ventricular filling by $30\%$.
3. **Ventricular Systole** ($0.3\text{ s}$): Ventricles contract; AV valves snap shut (**First Heart Sound: 'LUB'**); semilunar valves open, forcing blood into aorta and pulmonary artery.
4. **Ventricular Diastole**: Ventricles relax; semilunar valves snap shut (**Second Heart Sound: 'DUB'**).

$$
\text{Cardiac Output} = \text{Stroke Volume } (70\text{ mL}) \times \text{Heart Rate } (72\text{ bpm}) \approx 5000\text{ mL/min } (5\text{ L/min})
$$

## Electrocardiogram (ECG) Waves

* **P-wave**: Electrical depolarization of the atria.
* **QRS-complex**: Depolarization of the ventricles (triggers ventricular contraction).
* **T-wave**: Repolarization of the ventricles.

## Common Exam Traps

* **Heart Sound Mechanisms**: 'LUB' sound is caused by closure of **AV valves** (Tricuspid/Bicuspid); 'DUB' sound is caused by closure of **Semilunar valves**.
* **ECG Counting**: Counting the number of **QRS complexes** per unit time provides the exact heart rate of an individual.
