## title: Biodiversity and Conservation
level: intermediate → advanced

Biodiversity encompasses the variety of life across all levels of biological organization—genetic, species, and ecological diversity.

## Patterns of Biodiversity

* **Latitudinal Gradient**: Species diversity **decreases** as we move away from the equator toward the poles. Tropical regions host maximum species richness due to evolutionary stability, constant non-seasonal climate, and high solar radiation.
* **Species-Area Relationship**: Formulated by **Alexander von Humboldt**:

$$
\log S = \log C + Z \log A \quad \implies \quad S = CA^Z
$$

* $S$: Species richness, $A$: Area, $Z$: Slope of regression line ($0.1 - 0.2$ for small regions; $0.6 - 1.2$ for large continents).

```
 Species Richness (S)
       ^
       |                /--- S = CAᶻ
       |               /
       |          ----
       +-----------------------> Area (A)
```

## Threats to Biodiversity ("The Evil Quartet")

1. **Habitat Loss and Fragmentation**: Primary cause of extinction (e.g., destruction of Amazon Rain Forest, the "Lungs of the Planet").
2. **Over-exploitation**: Excessive human harvesting (e.g., extinction of Steller's sea cow, Passenger pigeon).
3. **Alien Species Invasions**: Introduced species displacing native flora/fauna (e.g., Nile Perch in Lake Victoria, *Parthenium*, Water Hyacinth/*Eichhornia*).
4. **Co-extinctions**: Obligate symbiotic pairs dying out together (e.g., fig tree and pollinator wasp).

## Conservation Strategies

* **In-situ Conservation (On-site)**: Protecting endangered species within their natural habitat. Includes **Biodiversity Hotspots** (34 globally; 3 in India: Western Ghats/Sri Lanka, Indo-Burma, Himalaya), National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Biosphere Reserves, and **Sacred Groves**.
* **Ex-situ Conservation (Off-site)**: Protecting species outside natural habitats in controlled setups. Includes Zoological Parks, Botanical Gardens, Seed Banks, and **Cryopreservation** (storage in liquid nitrogen at $-196^\circ\mathrm{C}$).

## Common Exam Traps

* **In-situ vs Ex-situ Classification**: **Sacred Groves** and **Biosphere Reserves** are *In-situ*; **Botanical Gardens**, **Zoological Parks**, and **Cryopreservation** are *Ex-situ*.
* **Cryopreservation Temperature**: Gametes and tissues are preserved in liquid nitrogen at exactly **$-196^\circ\mathrm{C}$**.

## Quiz

1. On a logarithmic scale, the species-area relationship plotted by Alexander von Humboldt yields a straight line described by which equation?

* \[ \] $S = C + Z A$
* \[x\] $\log S = \log C + Z \log A$
* \[ \] $\log S = Z \log C + A$
* \[ \] $S = CA + Z$

> Humboldt showed that on a logarithmic scale, the species-area relationship forms a straight line represented by $\log S = \log C + Z \log A$.

2. Cryopreservation of pollen grains, seeds, and gametes of threatened species in liquid nitrogen at $-196^\circ\text{C}$ is classified under which conservation strategy?

* \[ \] In-situ conservation
* \[x\] Ex-situ conservation
* \[ \] Biosphere reserve protection
* \[ \] Sacred grove preservation

> Cryopreservation preserves biological material outside natural habitats in advanced lab setups, making it an Ex-situ conservation technique.

3. Which factor belonging to "The Evil Quartet" is considered the single most important cause driving animal and plant species to extinction worldwide?

* \[x\] Habitat loss and fragmentation
* \[ \] Alien species invasion
* \[ \] Co-extinction
* \[ \] Over-exploitation for commercial gain

> Habitat loss and fragmentation is the leading cause of biodiversity loss, exemplified by the clearing of tropical rain forests.
