# Economics and Business syllabus plan (50 lessons, foundation to university entrance-exam level)

**Phase 1: Foundations (Lessons 1–12):** Introduction to economics and business, meaning and scope of economics, basic economic problems and scarcity, needs, wants and resources, factors of production, opportunity cost and production possibility curves, economic systems and resource allocation, demand and the law of demand, supply and the law of supply, market equilibrium and price determination, elasticity of demand and supply, introduction to business organisations, forms of business ownership, entrepreneurship and the role of businesses in the economy, and fundamental economic and business reasoning.

**Phase 2: Intermediate economics and business (Lessons 13–26):** Introduction to microeconomics and macroeconomics, production, productivity and costs, revenue, profit and break-even analysis, market structures including perfect competition and monopoly, monopolistic competition and oligopoly, consumer behaviour and utility theory, production decisions and economies of scale, national income and its measurement, gross domestic product and related economic indicators, economic growth and development, inflation and deflation, unemployment and labour markets, money and banking, central banking and monetary policy, government revenue and expenditure, fiscal policy, taxation and public debt, and international trade fundamentals.

**Phase 3: Advanced economics and business analysis (Lessons 27–40):** Economic fluctuations and business cycles, aggregate demand and aggregate supply, advanced market equilibrium and welfare economics, externalities and market failure, public goods and government intervention, income distribution and inequality, poverty and economic development strategies, international trade theories and trade policies, balance of payments and foreign exchange markets, globalisation and international business, business planning and strategic management, organisational structure and departmentalisation, human resource management and leadership theories, advanced marketing strategy and consumer analysis, financial management and investment decisions, financial statements and ratio analysis, business risk management and corporate governance, and entrepreneurship, innovation and business growth strategies.

**Phase 4: Examination mastery and case-study practice (Lessons 41–50):** Advanced microeconomic and macroeconomic problem-solving, advanced demand, supply and elasticity analysis, market structures and strategic business decision-making, national income and macroeconomic policy analysis, monetary and fiscal policy applications, international trade and foreign exchange problem-solving, financial statement interpretation and business calculations, integrated economic and business case-study analysis, previous-year university entrance examination questions and analysis, full-length Economics and Business mock examination I, and full-length Economics and Business mock examination II with comprehensive solutions and performance analysis.

The sequence begins with fundamental economic reasoning, scarcity, markets, demand, supply and basic business concepts. It progressively develops an understanding of microeconomics, macroeconomics, business operations, finance, marketing, management and international trade. The advanced phase introduces economic models, policy analysis, market failure, strategic management, financial interpretation and complex business decision-making. The final phase emphasizes quantitative problem-solving, analytical reasoning, real-world business cases and time-constrained examination practice for university entrance and competitive examinations such as CUET and other economics-, commerce- and business-related admission tests. Check the latest official syllabus for the examination being attempted; this learning plan is a broad integrated Economics and Business study pathway, not an official examination syllabus.
